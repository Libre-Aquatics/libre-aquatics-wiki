This article is a stub. Hy-Tek Business Manager, announced in 1989 under the fuller name
Swim Business Manager, was the club-accounting program in the Hy-Tek family, covering meet
entry fees, annual dues, invoicing of families and swimmers, and the tax records a team had
to keep, for a club of up to 99 groups across as many as four pools.

It is named in Hy-Tek advertising beside [Meet Manager](hy-tek-meet-manager.md) and
[Team Manager](hy-tek-team-manager.md) from 1989 but does not appear in the current product
line; see the [software overview](index.md) for what this section covers.
