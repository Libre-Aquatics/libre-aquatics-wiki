This article is a stub. PC Display Multisport, also written PCD Multisport, is
[ALGE-Timing](../../vendors/alge-timing.md) software that turns data from timing devices and from
[SwimTime](../meet-management/swimtime.md) into designs for video walls and PC screens.

See the [software overview](../index.md) for the other programs and interchange methods this wiki
covers.
