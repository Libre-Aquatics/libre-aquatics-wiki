This article is a stub. Team Connect Online is a Hy-Tek subscription service that keeps a
master copy of a club's [Team Manager](hy-tek-team-manager.md) database on Hy-Tek's server,
so coaches and volunteers on different computers can share one set of records instead of
exchanging backups.

It has been included free with every Team Manager 8.0 package, and it is separate from the
publishing service Team Stats Online; see the [software overview](index.md) for what this
section covers.
