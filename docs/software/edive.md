This article is a stub. eDive is a diving meet management program from
[CleanEntries](../vendors/cleanentries.md) that runs a meet's entries, dive sheets, scoring and
results from a laptop, takes judges' marks wirelessly, and posts live results to the web.

Daktronics names it, beside [DiveMeets](divemeets.md), as a results program that its
[OmniSport Pro Diving](omnisport-pro-diving.md) software exchanges meet data with.
