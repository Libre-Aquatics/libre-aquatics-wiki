Libre Aquatics Team is a planned open-source team- and roster-management application
for aquatics clubs and school programs, managing athletes, entries, and best times,
and exchanging entry and result files with meet-management software.

It is part of the [Libre Aquatics project](../about/index.md) alongside
[Libre Aquatics Meet](libre-aquatics-meet.md) and this reference, [Libre Aquatics Wiki](../index.md).

## Goals

- Open source and free for clubs and programs of any size.
- Clean import/export of entries and results with meet-management tools.
- Athlete, roster, and best-time tracking that is easy to keep current.
