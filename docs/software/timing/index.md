Programs that run a timing system from a computer, or that work beside a dedicated console
while a race is being timed. Most timing consoles are dedicated hardware, covered under
[timers](../../equipment/swimming/timers/index.md).

## What lives here

- Programs that put the console on a laptop, with a small box taking the signals from the
  deck.
- Race programs that run alongside a console or a hand-held timer.
- Services and utilities that link a timing program to a meet computer.

## Articles

### Superior Swim Timing

- [SST timing software](sst-timing-software.md): the laptop program from
  [Superior Swim Timing](../../vendors/superior-swim-timing.md) that does the job of a
  console.
- [SST Gateway](sst-gateway.md): passes schedule and results files to the SST console over
  the network.
- [SST App Server](sst-app-server.md) and [SST Cloud Relay](sst-cloud-relay.md): send the
  scoreboard feed to phones and tablets, locally and over the internet.
- [SST Stopwatch](sst-stopwatch.md): a phone app that sends lane times to the SST console.

### Swiss Timing

- [TOP-8](top-8.md): the timing program for the PowerTime and PowerTime II hand-held timers.
- [ARES-Print](ares-print.md): prints race results for the ARES 21 system.
- [ARES Water Polo](ares-water-polo.md): the water polo program for the ARES 21 system.

### Other programs

- [OmniSport Pro Swimming](omnisport-pro-swimming.md): a Daktronics program that runs swim
  races alongside an OmniSport 2000.
- [EasyFW](easyfw.md): times open water swims on a PC for
  [EasyWk](../meet-management/easywk.md).
- [TIMEWARE3](timeware3.md): uses a computer and an interface from
  [International Sports Timing](../../vendors/international-sports-timing.md) as a timing
  console.
- [com0com](com0com.md) and [HW VSP3](hw-vsp3.md): virtual serial port programs used in
  connection with the SST software.
