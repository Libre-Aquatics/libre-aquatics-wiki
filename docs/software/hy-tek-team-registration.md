This article is a stub. Team Registration for Swimming is one of the five swimming products
Hy-Tek currently lists, covering a club's sign-up and registration rather than the running
of a competition.

Nothing in the swimming press held for this wiki names it, so what is recorded here comes
from the vendor's own current product listing; see the [software overview](index.md) for
what this section covers.
