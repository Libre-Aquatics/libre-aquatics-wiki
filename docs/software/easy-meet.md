This article is a stub. Easy Meet was the meet-management program sold by EasyWare of
Milford, Ohio, advertised in the swimming press from 1986 to at least 2007 alongside a
companion Easy Team Manager.

It is the rival that Swimming World editorial pairs with
[Meet Manager](hy-tek-meet-manager.md) as the two programs a meet host would be running;
see the [software overview](index.md) for what this section covers.
