This article is a stub. Championship Meet Management was the meet-management program of
Championship Technologies of Riverside, Rhode Island, written by Bob Clauson, which the
company said had started on a university mainframe and accepted data from Colorado, Omega
and Daktronics timing consoles.

Its 1991 product copy claims it ran every NCAA national championship of the preceding decade
and that the company built the first automatic timing-to-computer interface with Colorado
Time Systems in 1982, claims repeated nowhere else in the sources held here; see the
[software overview](index.md) for what this section covers.
