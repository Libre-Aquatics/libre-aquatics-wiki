This article is a stub. Team Manager Online was Hy-Tek's web-hosted companion to
[Team Manager](hy-tek-team-manager.md), advertised from the early 2000s as a searchable
database with online meet entry, and still named as a product when
[The Active Network](../vendors/active-network.md) bought the company in 2008.

Hy-Tek had been advertising web-based entry under one name or another since 1999, and by
2003 it was the only company listed in Swimming World's online meet registration category;
see the [software overview](index.md) for what this section covers.
