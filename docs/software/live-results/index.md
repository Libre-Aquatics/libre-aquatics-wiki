Applications that show a meet's results to spectators while it is running, and the databases
that collect results afterwards.

## What lives here

- Live results, the applications and screens fed from meet or timing software.
- Rankings databases, the sites that hold results across meets.

## Articles

- [Meet Mobile](meet-mobile.md): The Active Network's phone application for heat sheets and
  results, fed from [Hy-Tek Meet Manager](../meet-management/hy-tek-meet-manager.md).
- [SplashMe](splashme.md): start lists and results from meets run on
  [Splash Meet Manager](../meet-management/splash-meet-manager.md).
- [SST Scoreboard App](sst-scoreboard-app.md): a spectator app fed from the
  [SST App Server](../timing/sst-app-server.md).
- [Swimnerd Live](swimnerd-live.md): live results on internet-connected screens.
- [Swimrankings](swimrankings.md): the results and rankings database that issues the meet,
  club and athlete identifiers carried in [Lenex](../file-formats/lenex.md) files.
- [Swimcloud](swimcloud.md): a results and rankings website that takes Hy-Tek exports, CL2
  and SDIF files.
