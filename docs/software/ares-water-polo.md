This article is a stub. ARES Water Polo, part number `3330.635`, is the Swiss Timing program for
water polo on the [ARES 21](../equipment/swimming/timers/ares-21.md) system, which Swiss Timing's
shot clock kit could be cabled to in place of a Saturn console.

See the [software overview](index.md) for the other programs the wiki covers.
