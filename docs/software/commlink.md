This article is a stub. Commlink was Hy-Tek's own data-exchange product, advertised from
1988, which moved entries and results between [Team Manager](hy-tek-team-manager.md) and
[Meet Manager](hy-tek-meet-manager.md) on a diskette posted between a club and a meet host.

By 1990 the same exchange could go by modem instead of by post, and through the decade
Commlink was treated as an alternative to [SDIF](sdif.md), the published standard, rather
than as an implementation of it, with governing bodies and magazines accepting times in
either; see the [software overview](index.md) for what this section covers.
