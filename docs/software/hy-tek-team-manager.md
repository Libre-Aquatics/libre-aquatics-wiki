This article is a stub. Hy-Tek Team Manager is the club-side program in the Hy-Tek pair,
holding a team's roster, its swimmers' best times and its meet entries, and it has been sold
alongside [Meet Manager](hy-tek-meet-manager.md) since 1986.

Entries made in Team Manager are what a meet host loads into Meet Manager to seed a
competition, a transfer that [Commlink](commlink.md) once carried on a diskette and that
[HY3](hy3.md) and [CL2](cl2.md) files carry now; see the [software overview](index.md) for
what this section covers.
