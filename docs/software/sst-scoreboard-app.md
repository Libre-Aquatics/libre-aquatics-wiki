This article is a stub. The SST Scoreboard App, released in September 2026, is a spectator app from
[Superior Swim Timing](../vendors/superior-swim-timing.md) that shows live race information from
the [SST App Server](sst-app-server.md), locally or through
[SST Cloud Relay](sst-cloud-relay.md).

See the [SST timing software](sst-timing-software.md) article for the other scoreboard outputs.
