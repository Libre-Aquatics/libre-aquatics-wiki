This article is a stub. LSA SwimMeet and SwimTeam were meet-management and team-management
programs for IBM PCs sold by [Lab Software Associates](../vendors/lab-software-associates.md),
advertised from 1985 and moved to Windows by 1997. Colorado Time Systems named it in 1996 among
the meet programs its [DisplayLink](displaylink.md) scoreboard software worked with.

See the [software overview](index.md) for the other programs this wiki covers.
