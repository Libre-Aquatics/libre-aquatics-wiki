This article is a stub. EasyWk is meet-management software for swimming by Björn Stickan, a
German referee, begun in 1999 and first sold in October 2000, and it is one of the programs
the Swimrankings wiki lists as supporting [Lenex](lenex.md).

Germany also has its own [DSV-Standard](dsv-standard.md) exchange format; see the
[software overview](index.md) for what this section covers.
