This article is a stub. Swimcloud Meet is the meet-management product from
[Swimcloud](swimcloud.md), which can exchange schedule and results files with the
[SST timing software](sst-timing-software.md) through a shared folder.

See the [software overview](index.md) for the other programs this wiki covers.
