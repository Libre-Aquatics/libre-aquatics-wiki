This article is a stub. Team Websites is the web-publishing product in Hy-Tek's current
swimming line, giving a club a site of its own rather than doing anything with a
competition.

It sits outside what this wiki otherwise covers and is recorded here so that the Hy-Tek
product line is complete; see the [software overview](index.md) for what this section
covers.
