This article is a stub. SST Cloud Relay is a subscription add-on to the
[SST App Server](sst-app-server.md) that publishes live race information over the internet,
where spectators reach it in the [SST Scoreboard App](sst-scoreboard-app.md) with a six-character
meet code.

See the [SST timing software](sst-timing-software.md) article for the other scoreboard outputs.
