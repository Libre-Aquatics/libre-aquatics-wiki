This article is a stub. Coach Card is an Integrated Sports Systems program in which artistic
swimming coaches prepare the declaration that World Aquatics rules have required for each routine
since 2022, listing its elements with their difficulty, for loading into
[SWA Scoring Manager](swa-scoring-manager.md) or [ISS MMS Artistic Swimming](iss-mms-artistic-swimming.md).

See the [software overview](index.md) for the other programs the wiki covers.
