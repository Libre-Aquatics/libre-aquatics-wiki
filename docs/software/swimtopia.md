This article is a stub. SwimTopia is a web-based swim team management service from Team
Topia, Inc., begun for summer-league teams, which handles registration, communication,
entries and results and can import a club's history from a
[Hy-Tek Team Manager](hy-tek-team-manager.md) database.

Its meet-management product is covered on the [Meet Maestro](meet-maestro.md) page; see the
[software overview](index.md) for what this section covers.
