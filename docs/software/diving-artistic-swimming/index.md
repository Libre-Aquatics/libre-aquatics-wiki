Programs that run and score judged aquatic competitions. They hold the entries and dive
sheets or routines, collect the judges' marks, and calculate the result.

## What lives here

- Meet programs for diving and for artistic swimming.
- Scoring programs that take marks from judges' terminals, consoles or tablets.
- Programs used beside them, for judges' entry, coaches' declarations, results display and
  results archives.

## Articles

### Integrated Sports Systems

[Integrated Sports Systems](../../vendors/integrated-sports-systems.md) (ISS) writes programs
under its own name, and two that [Swiss Timing](../../vendors/swiss-timing.md) sells.

| Program | Sport | Role |
|---|---|---|
| [DIV Scoring Manager](div-scoring-manager.md) | Diving | Runs a competition from entries and dive sheets to final results; sold by Swiss Timing |
| [SWA Scoring Manager](swa-scoring-manager.md) | Artistic swimming | Holds timetable, entries, panels and draws, and scores routines; sold by Swiss Timing |
| [ISS MMS Artistic Swimming](iss-mms-artistic-swimming.md) | Artistic swimming | The ISS meet and scoring program of which SWA Scoring Manager is an edition |
| [Run Event](run-event.md) | Diving | Runs an event at the scoring table |
| [i-Judge](i-judge.md) | Both | Judges' award entry on a computer or tablet |
| [Coach Card](coach-card.md) | Artistic swimming | Coaches' declaration of a routine's elements |
| [Display Results](display-results.md) | Both | Shows an event on screens and scoreboards while it is scored |
| [Rezman](rezman.md) | Both | Results archive and analysis service |

### Daktronics

- [OmniSport Pro Diving](omnisport-pro-diving.md): scores diving meets on a computer linked
  to an OmniSport 2000; no longer sold.
- [OmniSport Pro Synchro](omnisport-pro-synchro.md): scores synchronized swimming on a
  computer linked to the same console.

### Swiss Timing

- [ARES Diving](ares-diving.md) and
  [ARES Synchronized Swimming](ares-synchronized-swimming.md): the scoring programs of the
  ARES 21 system.

### Other programs

- [SynchroMM](synchromm.md) and [Gen7 Artistic Swimming](gen7-artistic-swimming.md): Colorado
  Time Systems programs for artistic swimming.
- [DiveMeets](divemeets.md): a diving entry and results website with a desktop program for
  running the meet.
- [eDive](edive.md): a diving meet program from CleanEntries.
- [ieDive](iedive.md) and [OmadaTrak](omadatrak.md): diving meet programs that Colorado Time
  Systems lists as compatible with its Gen7 Diving software.
