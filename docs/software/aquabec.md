This article is a stub. Aquabec was swimming competition-management software written by
Christian Berger in 1984 and rolled out through the Fédération de natation du Québec to
clubs across the province, and its files were still one of the formats a European meet
program advertised as supported in 2002.

It is named beside [Lenex](lenex.md) and [SDIF](sdif.md) in that advertisement; see the
[software overview](index.md) for what this section covers.
