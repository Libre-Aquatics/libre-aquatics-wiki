This article is a stub. Rezman is the results management and analysis service of
[Integrated Sports Systems](../vendors/integrated-sports-systems.md), which keeps past competition
results for queries by athlete, team and judge and receives the results of sanctioned Canadian
diving meets scored with [DIV Scoring Manager](div-scoring-manager.md).

See the [software overview](index.md) for the other programs the wiki covers.
