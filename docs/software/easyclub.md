This article is a stub. EasyClub is the club administration and best-times program that Björn
Stickan sells alongside [EasyWk](easywk.md), which manages a club's swimmers, officials,
entries and personal bests and exchanges DSV entry and results files, and which has been closed
to new licences since December 2024 in favor of his browser-based WebClub.

See the [software overview](index.md) for the other programs the wiki covers.
