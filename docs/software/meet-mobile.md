This article is a stub. Meet Mobile is a phone application from
[The Active Network](../vendors/active-network.md) that publishes a swim meet's heat sheets
and results while the meet is running, fed from the same company's
[Meet Manager](hy-tek-meet-manager.md).

Active acquired it rather than writing it, and it is the spectator-facing end of a chain
whose other end is the timing console on the deck; see the
[software overview](index.md) for what this section covers.
