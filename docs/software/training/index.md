Programs used in practice rather than in competition, for building workouts, recording times
and keeping training records.

## What lives here

- Workout programs, which build a session and send it to a pace clock or a console.
- Deck recording, the programs that time swims or save results during practice.
- Training records kept for one swimmer.

## Articles

- [Hy-Tek Workout Manager](hy-tek-workout-manager.md): builds interval sessions for a pace
  clock or console; announced in 1989, now an option of
  [Team Manager](../team-management/hy-tek-team-manager.md).
- [Hy-Tek Pro SWIMMER](hy-tek-pro-swimmer.md): a Palm OS program for hand times, splits and
  stroke rates on deck; no longer sold.
- [Hy-Tek Personal Swim Manager](hy-tek-personal-swim-manager.md): a training diary for an
  individual swimmer, announced in 1999.
- [QSTART](qstart.md): a Colorado Time Systems DOS program that logged results from the
  [QuickStart](../../equipment/common/pace-clock/quickstart.md) training display.
- [Swim Training](powertime-swim-training.md): a Swiss Timing program that times practice
  swims on the [PowerTime II](../../equipment/swimming/timers/powertime-ii.md).
