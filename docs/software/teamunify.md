This article is a stub. TeamUnify is a web-based swim club management service, founded in
2007 in Bend, Oregon, by Tom Fristoe, which SportsEngine bought in 2016 and which
PlayMetrics acquired with SportsEngine in 2026.

At the 2016 sale the companies gave its reach as about 4,000 swimming organizations, making
it the largest web-based rival to [Hy-Tek Team Manager](hy-tek-team-manager.md) on the club
side; see the [software overview](index.md) for what this section covers.
