This article is a stub. Computerized Swim League was a software package published by
Hy-Tek, Ltd. and attested once, in June 1984, when Swimming World reported it being donated
with hardware to the USA Olympic coach, its functions given as predicting events, graphing
and recording swimmers' times, keeping a coach's notebook and analysing records.

The name does not appear again in the magazines held for this wiki, and by 1986 Hy-Tek was
advertising [Meet Manager](hy-tek-meet-manager.md) and
[Team Manager](hy-tek-team-manager.md) instead; see the [software overview](index.md) for
what this section covers.
