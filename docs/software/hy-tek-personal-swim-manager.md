This article is a stub. Personal Swim Manager was a Hy-Tek program sold to an individual
swimmer rather than to a club, announced in 1999 as a computerised training diary that
tracked workouts, meet times and goals on a calendar, graphed progress, and could import
sessions from a [Workout Manager](hy-tek-workout-manager.md) database.

Swimming World reviewed it in April 2000, the only signed independent review of a Hy-Tek
product in the magazines held here, praising its range but calling it hard going for young
swimmers and noting it would not run on a Macintosh; see the
[software overview](index.md) for what this section covers.
