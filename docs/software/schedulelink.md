This article is a stub. ScheduleLink is a Colorado Time Systems add-on for its
[DisplayLink](displaylink.md)-driven matrix displays, which the company said scheduled
advertisements and messages onto the board.

It survives only in the archived product pages for the
[Myriad](../equipment/common/scoreboard/myriad.md) and
[Mercury16](../equipment/common/scoreboard/mercury16.md), and no Colorado Time Systems
manual held describes it.
