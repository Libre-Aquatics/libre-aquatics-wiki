This article is a stub. CPS-Schwimm is a networked Windows program for running swimming
competitions, published by Hermann Josef Aretz of Herzogenrath, Germany, and listed by the
Swimrankings wiki among the programs that support [Lenex](lenex.md).

Germany also has its own [DSV-Standard](dsv-standard.md) exchange format; see the
[software overview](index.md) for what this section covers.
