This article is a stub. SST Stopwatch is an iOS and Android app from
[Superior Swim Timing](../vendors/superior-swim-timing.md), with a version history going back to
2015, that sends lane times over Wi-Fi to the SST console and does not work with other makers'
timing systems.

See the [SST timing software](sst-timing-software.md) article for how the console uses these times.
