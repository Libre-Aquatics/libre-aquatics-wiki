The scoreboards and display modules made only for water polo. The shared background on how a
scoreboard works is in the [scoreboards overview](../../common/scoreboard/index.md).

## What lives here

- Scoreboards that show the game clock, the score and the period.
- Penalty modules that add the time left on an exclusion.
- Portable displays that show the game time and shot time.

Multisport boards that also serve water polo, such as [Calypso](../../common/scoreboard/calypso.md),
are under [scoreboards](../../common/scoreboard/index.md).

## Articles
