This article is a stub. CTS makes its wall plate as a pre-wired 15 × 15 in (38.1 ×
38.1 cm) aluminium panel, set flush in the pool wall, where in-deck wiring from the deck plates
ends and where the timing console, start system and scoreboard connect.

See the [deck cabling overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
deck plate and harness.
