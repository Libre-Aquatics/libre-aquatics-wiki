This article is a stub. The Daktronics wall plate is where an
[OmniSport 2000](../timers/omnisport-2000.md) console connects to an in-deck pool's wiring, with
near-end lane, start, lane speaker and scoreboard connections on the single-ended version and
far-end, real-time data (RTD) and Ethernet connections added on the double-ended one, and the
[lane interface](daktronics-lane-interface.md) mounted behind it.

See the [deck cabling overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
deck plate and harness.
