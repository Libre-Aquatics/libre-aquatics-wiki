This article is a stub. The Quantum harness cable is the Swiss Timing Tuchel 7-pin cable,
`1892.025` at 25 m with 50 m, 75 m and 100 m versions on drums, that joins the first
[Quantum Mobile Harness](quantum-mobile-harness.md) module at a pool end to the
[Quantum Aquatics](../timers/quantum.md) timer's harness input.

See the [deck cabling overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
deck plate and harness.
