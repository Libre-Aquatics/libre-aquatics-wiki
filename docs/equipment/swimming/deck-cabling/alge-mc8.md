This article is a stub. The MC8 is the [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) cable that
links eight lane finish push buttons to a [Timy](../timers/alge-timy.md) in ALGE's semi-automatic
swimming system, named `MC8-SWIM` in the 2013 catalogue and `MC8-S2` in the 2026 one.

See the [deck-cabling overview](index.md) for the shared background on deck cabling.
