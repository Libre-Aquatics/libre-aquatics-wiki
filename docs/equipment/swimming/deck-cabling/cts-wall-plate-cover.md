This article is a stub. The Colorado Time Systems wall plate cover, `C-WLP-01` for new plates and
`C-WLP-02` for retrofit to older ABS plates, is a lockable 16 × 16 × 2 in anodised aluminium box
that closes over a [CTS wall plate](cts-wall-plate.md) and needs 20 in of clearance to open.

See the [deck cabling overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
deck plate and wall plate.
