Swimming installation guide E-97-03-24:
  - One FIN1 for every two lanes; it reaches the S4-SWIM over a 5-core cable 078.
  - Each FIN1 takes two touchpads and their handswitches. The pad goes in the red banana socket and
    the handswitch in the blue one; both sockets are labelled "TOUCH PAD" in the guide's text,

  - Left-hand sockets serve the odd lanes (1, 3, 5, 7) and right-hand sockets the even lanes.
  - For a fixed installation without ALGE starting blocks, ALGE advised fitting each FIN1 in a

  - The false-start drawing (SCHW-E05.DWG, 3 November 1994) labels the adapters "FIN1R, FIN2R" in

  - Handswitch on the drawings is 023-10 (see alge-023-02.md).

Not established: dimensions, whether FIN1R/FIN2R are separate models, dates.

This article is a stub. The `FIN1` is the [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) finish
adapter that connects the touchpads and handswitches of two lanes to an
[S4-SWIM](alge-s4-swim.md) lane box, odd lanes on its left side and even lanes on its right.

See the [deck-cabling overview](index.md) for the shared background on lane wiring.
