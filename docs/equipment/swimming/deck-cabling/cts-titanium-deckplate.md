This article is a stub. The Titanium Deckplate (`TDP`) is a Colorado Time Systems in-deck plate
whose titanium jacks sit on a domed top that sheds water from between the contacts, a design
covered by US Patent 8,602,815, and it can replace the company's older 7.5 in square `DP-7`
plates.

See the [deck cabling overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
deck plate and harness.
