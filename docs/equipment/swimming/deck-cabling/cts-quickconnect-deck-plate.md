This article is a stub. The QuickConnect deck plate is a Colorado Time Systems in-deck
connection that sets a military-style connector with gold-plated contacts in the deck and moves
the lane's jacks to a connection hub on the starting block, joined to the deck connector by one
short cable.

See the [deck cabling overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
deck plate and harness.
