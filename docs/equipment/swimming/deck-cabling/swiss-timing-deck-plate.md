This article is a stub. The Swiss Timing deck plate (`3493.603`) is a 153 mm square plate set
flush in the pool deck, with jacks for a touchpad, a relay platform, three pushbuttons and
loudspeakers wired for both a primary and a secondary timer, and its cable runs to an
[ODB10-SW](odb10-sw.md) distribution box.

See the [deck cabling overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
deck plate and harness.
