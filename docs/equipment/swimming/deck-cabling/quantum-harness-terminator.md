This article is a stub. The Quantum harness terminator (`3480.770`) is the Swiss Timing plug
that must be fitted to the last module of a [Quantum Mobile Harness](quantum-mobile-harness.md)
chain, and one comes with every harness set.

See the [deck cabling overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
deck plate and harness.
