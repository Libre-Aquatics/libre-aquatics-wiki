This article is a stub. Swiss Timing wall sockets are surface-mounted cast-aluminium boxes,
with one or two 4-pole or 7-pole Tuchel plugs, that bring signals from fixed timing wiring,
such as the start line from an [ODB10-SW](odb10-sw.md) distribution box, out to the poolside.

See the [deck cabling overview](index.md) for the shared background on fixed in-deck wiring.
