This article is a stub. The ODB10 is a Swiss Timing distribution box for fixed pool cabling
that, according to a distributor listing, gathered the deck-plate wiring of up to ten lanes for
a primary and a backup [ARES 21](../timers/ares-21.md) timer and is no longer supplied.
Its listed size and weight differ from those of its Quantum-era successor, the
[ODB10-SW](odb10-sw.md).

See the [deck cabling overview](index.md) for the shared background on fixed in-deck wiring.
