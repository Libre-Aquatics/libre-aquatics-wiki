This article is a stub. Cable `309-2.5` is the 2.5 m [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md)
lead that joins a [TP touchpad](../touchpad/alge-tp-touchpads.md) to a lane terminal such as the
[SWT3](alge-swt3.md).

See the [deck cabling overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every lane
connection.
