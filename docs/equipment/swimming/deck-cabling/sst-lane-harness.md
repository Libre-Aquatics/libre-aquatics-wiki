This article is a stub. The SST lane harness is a waterproof deck cable from
[Superior Swim Timing](../../../vendors/superior-swim-timing.md), sold in 6-, 8- and 10-lane
versions, that connects pads and buttons to the
[SST timer](../timers/sst-timer.md).

See the [deck cabling overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every harness.
