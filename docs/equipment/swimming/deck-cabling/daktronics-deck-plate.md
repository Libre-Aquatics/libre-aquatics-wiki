This article is a stub. Daktronics deck plates are the in-deck connection plates of an
[OmniSport 2000](../timers/omnisport-2000.md) installation: an individual lane plate with jacks
for the touchpad, three buttons, a lane speaker and a remote strobe, a start location plate for
the horn start and auxiliary speaker, and a bulkhead plate used in pairs.

See the [deck cabling overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
deck plate and harness.
