The DP-7 is the flat, 7.5 in square in-deck lane plate made by
[Colorado Time Systems](../../../vendors/colorado-time-systems.md) (CTS) before its
[Titanium Deckplate](cts-titanium-deckplate.md). One plate sits in the deck at each starting
block, and the lane's touchpad, three backup buttons, start line and lane speaker plug into it.
A pre-wired cable runs from it under the deck to the facility's
[wall plate](cts-wall-plate.md), where the timing console connects.[^tdpsheet][^arch] CTS's
architectural drawings label it as one of a "DP-XX" series, and the company offers the titanium
plate as a direct replacement in the same opening.[^arch][^tdpsheet]

## Role in the timing system

This section places the plate in a CTS installation. A pool wired in-deck has a plate at every
lane at each timed end, and officials plug the lane's equipment into it for a meet instead of
laying a [cable harness](cts-cable-harness.md) along the pool edge.[^arch] The plates at the
two outside lanes also carry a connection for the start system itself.[^arch] A System 5 or
System 6 console takes each end's in-deck cable on the same input a harness would use.[^f506][^f870]

CTS sold the DP-7 alongside the [QuickConnect deck plate](cts-quickconnect-deck-plate.md),
which moves the connections up onto the starting block. Its 2012 guidelines describe the two as
the regular and QuickConnect styles.[^arch] By 2015 the catalog listed only the Titanium
Deckplate and the QuickConnect for in-deck pools, and the DP-7 was not among
them.[^catalog2015]

See the [deck cabling overview](index.md) for the background shared by every deck plate:
deck-box placement, conduit sizing and the other manufacturers' layouts.

## Design and construction

CTS's drawing shows a square flat plate held by a screw at each corner. It has six labelled
positions, each with a pair of banana jacks angled toward the centre. Touchpad, button A and
button B run down the left side, and start, speaker and button C down the right.[^arch]

The plate has no position of its own for a relay judging platform or its speedlight. A platform
shares the button A jack instead: its cable ends in a stackable banana plug that goes into button
A, and the button A cable plugs into the back of that plug. If fewer than three buttons are in
use, the buttons move to B and C and the platform takes button A alone.[^f968][^f870] CTS's
2012 guidelines reserve platform speedlights for the QuickConnect system.[^arch] The relay
platform manual places a speedlight socket on a deck plate without saying which kind it
means.[^f968] The drawing of the standard plate has none.[^arch]

Each plate leaves the factory potted, with up to 200 ft (61 m) of cable already attached.[^arch]
The jacks sit flat on the face of the plate. CTS's later patent for the titanium plate describes
the weakness of that layout: splashed water can lie in a film across two contacts, and the small
current through it corrodes them.[^patentplate]

## Installation and mounting

This section covers what is specific to this plate. The plate's potting collar, 3.5 in across,
fits into a 4 × 4 × 6 in (10.2 × 10.2 × 15.2 cm) PVC deck box, one box per lane, and stainless
steel screws fix the plate down into the tile. A channel at the edge of the plate takes
silicone caulk. For the plate to lie flush, the box top and the finished tile may differ by no
more than 0.25 in.[^arch] In CTS's cross-section the box is joined to 2 in PVC conduit
with a male adaptor and locking washer on each side.[^arch]

The box sits beneath the block, in the space between its legs and the pool wall, with at least
3 in clear on every side for the plate. On a two-legged block it is centred between the
legs; on a single-post block it lines up with the mounting plate.[^arch] The
[Titanium Deckplate](cts-titanium-deckplate.md) fits the same opening, so a pool can be
converted without new deck boxes.[^tdpsheet]

## Connection to the timer

At the lane, touchpads and pushbuttons use ordinary two-pin banana plugs.[^f870] CTS's speedlight
relay platforms use cable [`R-015-027`](../timers/cts-r-015-027-cable.md), whose banana leg
stacks into the button A jack.[^f968] The start system connects through the start jacks on an
outside-lane plate or at the wall plate; CTS's 1997 start-system manual has the starter's timer
lead plugged in at the deck plate on an in-deck pool.[^f503][^f870]

Behind the plate, each lane's cable runs back through the conduit to the wall plate, which
gathers the lanes of each pool end onto a multi-pin socket for the console.[^arch] The
[Gen7 Legacy Timer](../timers/gen7-legacy.md) can read these plates too. Its diagnostics show
each deck plate input as open, closed, shorted or corroded, so a fault can be traced to the
device or to the jack.[^f1058]

## Care and maintenance

CTS has given three different cleaning routines for plates of this kind. The System 5 manual has the
jacks cleaned by letting some Lime-A-Way stand in them to loosen the corrosion, a cotton swab worked
in and out, a damp swab, and then silicone grease.[^f506] The System 6 manual keeps the same
steps but uses rubbing alcohol, and holds Lime-A-Way back for severe corrosion.[^f870] CTS's 2011
cleaning sheet reverses the first advice. It asks for dielectric grease in every jack before
each use, enough that some is pushed out when a plug goes in. After use, each jack is cleaned
with alcohol on a cotton pipe cleaner, rinsed in fresh water and greased again. Diluted
lime-scale remover is kept as a last resort and is never to be used routinely.[^f897] The 2014
touchpad manual repeats the 2011 routine.[^f147]

A plate can be checked with CTS's [`TPM-D` test meter](../timers/cts-tpm-d-test-meter.md). With
the timer connected and switched on, the meter goes into the plate's touchpad jack and then its
button jack. A reading of 4.5–5 V is good, and anything lower points to the timer, the plate or
a dirty connector. CTS says to clean the plate and test again before calling support.[^meter]

## Compared with the Titanium Deckplate

The [Titanium Deckplate](cts-titanium-deckplate.md) keeps the 7.5 in square face so that it can
replace the DP-7 directly. It moves each jack onto a raised lobe of a domed top so that water
drains away, uses titanium contacts, and gives the relay platform and its speedlight their own
three-pin position.[^tdpsheet] Its cleaning sheet drops the greasing routine and asks only that
debris be removed before use.[^f1023] CTS also gives the titanium plate a 5-year warranty.[^tdpsheet]
CTS's general warranty on deck plates was shorter: two years in 1997, and one year by
2014.[^f503][^warranty]
