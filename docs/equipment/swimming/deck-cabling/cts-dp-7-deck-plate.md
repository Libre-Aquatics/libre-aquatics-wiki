This article is a stub. The `DP-7` is the older 7.5 in square Colorado Time Systems in-deck
lane plate, whose banana jacks CTS asked facilities to grease before every use and which the
company offers to replace in the same opening with the
[Titanium Deckplate](cts-titanium-deckplate.md).

See the [deck cabling overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
deck plate and harness.
