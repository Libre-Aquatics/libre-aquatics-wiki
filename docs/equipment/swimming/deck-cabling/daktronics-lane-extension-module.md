This article is a stub. The Daktronics lane extension module, Lane Extension Module II
(`0A-1056-0150`), is the lead that runs from an OmniSport 2000 console's J10 or J11 socket to
the TO NEXT LANE jacks of the nearest [lane module](daktronics-lane-module.md), made in 25, 50,
100 and 200 ft (7.6, 15.2, 30.5 and 61 m) lengths.

See the [deck cabling overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
deck plate and harness.
