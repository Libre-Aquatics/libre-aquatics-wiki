This article is a stub. The OCP5 is the Omega-branded touchpad made by
[Swiss Timing](../../../vendors/swiss-timing.md), built from PVC slats on a stainless steel frame
in four sizes from 150 × 60 cm to 240 × 90 cm, and connected to a
[Quantum Aquatics](../timers/quantum.md) timer through its lane harness.

See the [touchpads overview](index.md) for the shared background.
