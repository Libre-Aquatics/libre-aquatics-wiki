This article is a stub. The TP touchpads (`TP2400C`, `TP1890C`, `TP2300C`) are
[ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) swimming touchpads with a stainless-steel casing,
PVC lamellas and four tape switches, connected to a [SWT3
terminal](../deck-cabling/alge-swt3.md) by banana plugs.

See the [touchpads overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every touchpad.
