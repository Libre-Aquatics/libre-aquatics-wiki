This article is a stub. The `VP-2` is the Colorado Time Systems trigger vacuum pump and needle,
documented from 2009 and sold beside the [TPM-D test meter](../timers/cts-tpm-d-test-meter.md),
which adjusts the air inside a touchpad through the plug on its connector block, with a
vacuum/pressure switch the earlier [VP-1](cts-vp-1-vacuum-pump.md) lacked.

See the [touchpads overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
touchpad.
