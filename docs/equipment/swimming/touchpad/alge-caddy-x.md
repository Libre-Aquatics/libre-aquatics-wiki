This article is a stub. The `CADDY X` is an [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md)
stainless-steel trolley that holds up to 12 [TP touchpads](alge-tp-touchpads.md) for storage and
transport.

See the [touchpads overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every touchpad.
