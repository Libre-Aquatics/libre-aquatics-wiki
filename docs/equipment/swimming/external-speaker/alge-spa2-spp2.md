This article is a stub. The `SPA2` and `SPP2` are [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md)
lane speakers: a battery-backed active unit stands behind each odd lane of a
[TM-SWIM](../timers/alge-tm-swim.md) system and drives a passive unit behind the next even lane.

See the [external-speakers overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
lane speaker.
