Colorado Time Systems (CTS) equips its deck-wired
[start systems](../starter/index.md) with an under-block lane speaker cataloged as
`SP-6/45`: a 6 watt, 45 ohm reflex horn mounted behind each starting block. A
start system drives up to twelve of them, wired in parallel on one speaker
circuit, so the start tone and the starter's commands reach every lane
together.[^f875] The speaker itself is not made by CTS; it is the 306 Series
reflex horn made by [Fourjay Industries](#the-fourjay-306-horn) of Dayton,
Ohio, and CTS resells it under its own part number.[^fourjay306] The auxiliary,
far-lane, and underwater speakers CTS sells for the same start systems are
covered in each start system's article; shared background on lane speakers is
in the [external-speakers overview](index.md).

## Use across the CTS start line

The same 6 watt, 45 ohm under-block speaker runs through the CTS deck-wired
start systems:

- The [Championship Start System](../starter/championship-start-system.md) (`SS`)
  drives up to twelve `SP-6/45` speakers on its main circuit, wired through an
  `LS40` cable harness.[^f875]
- The [Championship Elite Start System](../starter/championship-elite-start-system.md)
  (`SSE`) uses the same speaker, cataloged `R-SP-6-45` with an attached cable, on an
  `R-LS40-x` harness.[^datasheet] The sources disagree on the cable length: the
  CTS datasheet lists a 26 ft cable, while the Poolweb dealer listing for the
  same part gives 26 inches and sells a separate 72 inch version
  (`R-SP-6-45-72`). The dealer figure is consistent with the 72 inch option
  being the long version.[^datasheet][^poolweb]
- The legacy [CHAMP-SSM](../starter/champ-ssm.md) generation drove up to twelve
  6 watt, 45 ohm under-block speakers in the same way.[^f875]
- The 1990s [SS2](../starter/ss2.md) offered six, eight, or ten 6 watt under-block
  speakers on the matching `LS40-6`, `LS40-8`, or `LS40-10` harness.[^f503]

The start tone on the lane-speaker circuit is fixed at full volume; only the
public-address level is adjustable.[^f875]

## The Fourjay 306 horn

The `SP-6/45` is the Fourjay 306-45, the 45 ohm version of Fourjay's 306 Series
"Little Big Horn" reflex speaker. Fourjay builds the 306 in two impedances, an
8 ohm `306-8` and the 45 ohm `306-45`; the 45 ohm version is the one used as a
lane speaker, because its high impedance lets a dozen speakers share one
amplifier circuit.[^fourjay306] Fourjay sells the 306 as a general-purpose
paging and talk-back horn, and electronics retailers stock the same 306-45 for
public-address work; the lane speaker is a standard commercial part rather than
an aquatics-specific design.[^pe306] The horn body and adjustable base are moulded from
impact- and UV-resistant ABS with stainless-steel fasteners, and the reflex driver
uses a moisture-resistant diaphragm, so the unit withstands the poolside
environment. It carries a 2-conductor cable, mounts to a 3-inch electrical box or
recesses through holes in the horn flange, and weighs about 1.25 lb.[^fourjay306]

## Specifications

The under-block speaker's ratings are Fourjay's for the 306-45; CTS does not
publish separate figures.[^fourjay306]

| | |
|---|---|
| CTS part number | `SP-6/45` (`R-SP-6-45` with attached cable) |
| OEM model | Fourjay 306-45 |
| Power handling | 6 W continuous |
| Impedance | 45 Ω |
| Frequency range | 400–5000 Hz (±3 dB); 320–6000 Hz (±6 dB) on axis |
| Sound pressure level | 97 dB average (1 W/1 m, 500–5000 Hz) |
| Driver | Reflex type, 3.5 in, moisture-resistant diaphragm |
| Construction | Impact- and UV-resistant ABS; stainless-steel fasteners; tan |
| Cable | 2-conductor, 18 gauge |
| Mounting | Adjustable base to a 3-inch electrical box; flange for recess mounting |
| Weight | 1.25 lb (0.57 kg) |

## Part numbers and accessories

- `SP-6/45`, 6 watt, 45 ohm under-block lane speaker (Fourjay 306-45);
  `R-SP-6-45`, the same speaker with an attached cable on the Elite (26 ft per
  the CTS datasheet, 26 in per the dealer listing); `R-SP-6-45-72`, the 72 in
  cable version.[^f875][^datasheet][^poolweb]
- `LS40-6` / `-8` / `-10` / `-12`, lane-speaker cable harness; the number is the
  lane count. `R-LS40-x` is the Elite equivalent for 6, 8, or 10 lanes.[^f875][^datasheet]
