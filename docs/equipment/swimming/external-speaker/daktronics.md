Daktronics, Inc., of Brookings, South Dakota, equips its
[HS-200 Horn Start](../starter/hs-200.md) with an under-block lane speaker, 4J
model `306-45-54` (catalog number `DS-1059`), one behind each starting block.
The HS-200 drives up to ten of them from a dedicated lane output with its own
sealed volume control.[^manual][^specsheet] As with the
[Colorado Time Systems](cts.md) equivalent, the horn itself is not made by
Daktronics: the `306-45` is the reflex horn made by Fourjay Industries of
Dayton, Ohio. The HS-200's auxiliary and backstroke speakers are covered in the
[HS-200](../starter/hs-200.md) article; shared background on lane speakers is in
the [external-speakers overview](index.md).

## Design and construction

The lane speaker is the Fourjay 306-45, the 45 ohm version of Fourjay's 306
Series reflex horn, the same horn Colorado Time Systems sells as its
`SP-6/45`.[^fourjay306] Daktronics designates it `306-45-54`; the owner's manual
does not define the trailing `54`, which most likely records a cable length but
is unconfirmed.[^manual] The 45 ohm impedance is what allows the several lane
speakers to share the lane output on the start system.[^fourjay306]

Daktronics installs each lane speaker tilted downward so that water drains out of
the horn rather than pooling inside it, and offers speaker extension cords in
lengths from 10 ft to 200 ft (3 m to 61 m) to reach the blocks.[^manual] The horn
is built from impact- and UV-resistant ABS with stainless-steel fasteners and a
moisture-resistant reflex driver.[^fourjay306]

## Specifications

The lane speaker's ratings are Fourjay's for the 306-45; Daktronics does not
publish separate figures.[^fourjay306]

| | |
|---|---|
| Daktronics designation | `306-45-54` (catalog `DS-1059`) |
| OEM model | Fourjay 306-45 |
| Type | Reflex horn |
| Power handling | 6 W continuous |
| Impedance | 45 Ω |
| Frequency range | 400–5000 Hz (±3 dB) |
| Sound pressure level | 97 dB (1 W/1 m) |
| Construction | Impact- and UV-resistant ABS; stainless-steel fasteners |
| Weight | 1.25 lb (0.57 kg) |

## Part numbers and accessories

- `306-45-54` (`DS-1059`), under-block lane speaker (Fourjay 306-45); up to ten
  per HS-200.[^manual]
- Speaker extension cords, 10 ft to 200 ft.[^manual]
