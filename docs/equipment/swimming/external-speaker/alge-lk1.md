This article is a stub. The `LK1` is the [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) start-signal
loudspeaker of the [Timer S4](../timers/alge-timer-s4.md) swimming system, drawn four to a
[Start Acoustic SA1](../starter/alge-sa1.md) with each speaker chained from the one before.

See the [external-speakers overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
external speaker.
