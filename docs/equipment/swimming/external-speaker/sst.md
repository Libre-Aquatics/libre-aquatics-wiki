[Superior Swim Timing](../../../vendors/superior-swim-timing.md) (SST) sells a lane speaker and matching multi-lane speaker
harnesses as accessories to its
[Atlantis Swimming Starter System](../starter/atlantis.md), which drives a full
set of lane speakers in addition to its built-in speaker.[^atlantis][^pricing]
The products are sold directly by SST and through SwimNerd, whose store lists
them alongside SST's timing systems.[^snspeaker][^snharness] SST does not
publish the lane speaker's wattage, impedance, or manufacturer, so this article
is limited to what the listings state. Shared background on lane speakers is in
the [external-speakers overview](index.md).

## Design and construction

The SST Lane Speaker is an under-block speaker that connects to the start
system through an SST speaker harness, sold in six-, eight-, and ten-lane
versions.[^pricing][^snharness] SST describes the speaker as delivering
high-output audio to each lane and the harness as a waterproof deck cable with
waterproof connectors, which the company says extends the service life of both
the harness and the speakers; these durability claims are the vendor's
own.[^snspeaker][^snharness] The speakers plug into the harness, and the
harness plugs into the start system.[^snspeaker]

## Specifications

SST publishes no electrical specifications for the lane speaker or harness: no
power rating, impedance, frequency range, sound-pressure level, or OEM. The
only cataloged facts are the product names and the harness lane
counts.[^pricing][^snspeaker] This distinguishes the line from the
[Colorado Time Systems](cts.md) and [Daktronics](daktronics.md) equivalents,
whose Fourjay OEM horn has a published datasheet.
