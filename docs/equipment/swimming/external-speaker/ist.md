[International Sports Timing](../../../vendors/international-sports-timing.md) (IST), a division of Industrial Service Technology,
Inc., sold its lane speakers as the Under Block Speaker System, an accessory to
its portable [SWIMSTART Electronic Start](../starter/swimstart.md).[^ubss] The
company described the system as putting a speaker under each block so that
every swimmer has the same advantage on the start.[^ubss] IST does not disclose
who makes the speaker or publish an impedance figure, and as of 2026 the
under-block products no longer appear in the company's current
catalog.[^cat40w] Shared background on lane speakers is in the
[external-speakers overview](index.md).

## Design and construction

The system was cataloged under SKU `SPKRBLOCK-6`, with the lane cable made in
six-, eight-, and ten-lane versions (`SPKRBLOCK-6`, `-8`, and
`-10`).[^ubss][^rep] The individual speaker, sold separately as a replacement
under SKU `SPEAKER6W`, is a 6 watt, 120 dB unit with a connecting cable that
plugs into the `SPKRBLOCK` lane cable and mounts underneath the starting
block.[^rep]

The product pages were live through 2024, but IST's current catalog no longer
lists the under-block system or the replacement speaker, so the line's status
is unclear.[^ubss][^cat40w]

## Specifications

IST publishes a power and sound-pressure figure but no impedance, frequency
range, or OEM for the lane speaker.

- `SPKRBLOCK-6` / `-8` / `-10`, under-block speaker system and lane cable for

- `SPEAKER6W`, replacement 6 watt, 120 dB under-block speaker.[^rep]
