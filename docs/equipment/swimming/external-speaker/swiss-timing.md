Swiss Timing Ltd, of Corgémont, Switzerland, is the Swatch Group timing company
whose equipment appears under the Omega brand at major international meets. It
places the lane speaker in the starting block rather than on an under-block horn
as the United States manufacturers do: a fixed installation fits an internal
loudspeaker inside the block, and a mobile installation uses a set of external
loudspeakers positioned near each block.[^osb11][^stconcept] Both are driven by
the [StartTime V](../starter/starttime-v.md) electronic starting device. Swiss
Timing does not disclose who makes the loudspeakers. Shared background on lane
speakers is in the [external-speakers overview](index.md).

## In-block loudspeakers

For fixed wiring, Swiss Timing offers an internal loudspeaker, article
3399.600, as an option fitted inside its OSB11 and OSB14 starting blocks. The
speaker connects with two banana plugs, and a door at the back of the block
gives access to the interior for the cabling.[^osb11][^osb14] The timing
system's other lane devices connect into a lane deck plate installed under the
centre of each block, which the datasheets say protects the cables, the
connections, and the speaker.[^osb11][^osb14]

Swiss Timing's installation manual calls these units lane speakers, and
presents the arrangement as the preferred configuration for an in-deck
installation: the cabling runs below deck, the start area stays clear of
clutter in line with FINA (now World Aquatics) facilities recommendations, and
the blocks can be removed between competitions without disturbing the permanent
wiring.[^stconcept]

## Mobile loudspeakers

Where the cabling is mobile rather than in-deck, Swiss Timing supplies external
loudspeakers, one set close to each block, the aim being that the start reaches every
swimmer at the same instant. They are sold as sets of three to ten speakers
with cable harnesses; the final digit pair of the article number records the
cable length between two loudspeakers, 3 m or 6 m.[^stvds][^stvman]

| Article | Set |
|---|---|
| 3399.933.06 | 3 mobile loudspeakers, 6 m cables |
| 3399.934.03 / .06 | 4 mobile loudspeakers, 3 m or 6 m cables |
| 3399.935.03 / .06 | 5 mobile loudspeakers, 3 m or 6 m cables |
| 3399.936.03 | 6 mobile loudspeakers, 3 m cables |
| 3399.938.03 | 8 mobile loudspeakers, 3 m cables |
| 3399.940.03 | 10 mobile loudspeakers, 3 m cables |

The installation manual illustrates the external loudspeaker as a unit of about
180 × 215 × 192 mm, and lists a multipurpose tripod (article 9051.6339,
rated to 8 kg) among the start-system options.[^stconcept][^stvds]

## Connections

The loudspeakers are driven by the [StartTime V](../starter/starttime-v.md)
starting device, which is covered in its own article. The lane wiring is set by
the amplifier's load window: the recommended load is between 3.2 and 16 ohms,
and any speaker combination inside that range is acceptable, so an installation
combines the per-lane loudspeakers to land within it. Speaker polarity must be
kept consistent across the installation or sound quality degrades.[^stvman]

## Specifications

Swiss Timing publishes article numbers, set sizes, and system-level electrical
limits, but no per-speaker wattage, impedance, or frequency figures.

| | |
|---|---|
| In-block loudspeaker | Article 3399.600; fixed wiring; 2 banana plugs; fits OSB11 and OSB14 |
| Mobile loudspeaker sets | 3 to 10 speakers, 3 m or 6 m harness cables (3399.933.06 to 3399.940.03) |
| Amplifier load | 3.2 to 16 Ω recommended; 2 Ω possible with distortion |
| Speaker cable runs | Up to 30 m at 0.75 mm²; up to 50 m at 1 or 1.5 mm² |
