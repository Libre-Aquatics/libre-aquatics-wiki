This article is a stub. The `BANG SPK` is an [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md)
passive speaker of about 10 W that extends a [BANG](alge-bang.md) speaker system, with
up to eight chained from its SPEAKER socket on fixed 12 m cables.

See the [timers overview](../timers/index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console.
