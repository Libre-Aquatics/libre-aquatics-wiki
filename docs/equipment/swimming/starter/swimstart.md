The SWIMSTART Electronic Start is a portable start system for swimming made by
[International Sports Timing](../../../vendors/international-sports-timing.md) (IST), a division of Industrial Service Technology,
Inc., of Grand Rapids, Michigan. It packages a public-address system, a start
tone, a 360 degree strobe, and a false-start recall tone in a battery-powered
case that sits on the deck or straps to the backstroke
flagpole.[^manual][^listing][^brochure] This article is a starting point; the
[start-systems overview](index.md) covers the shared background that applies to
every starter.

## Design and hardware

The unit ships with a 40 watt speaker, a microphone, and a charging
transformer. The speaker mounts on the right side of the case on a U-shaped
bracket, rotates on its base to set the horn orientation, and connects with a
polarized banana plug; the replacement part is `SPEAKER40W-M`, a 40 watt,
120 dB speaker whose listing gives a weight of 5 lb and dimensions of 11 by 9
by 7 inches.[^manual][^40w] A second socket under the speaker accepts auxiliary
speakers, and the unit supports two additional speakers.[^40w] IST bridges the
speaker's power amplifiers to increase output.[^brochure]

The case is aluminum with a black anodized finish, about 12 by 8 by 4 inches
and 23 pounds.[^brochure] The 360 degree strobe uses a 6,000 volt xenon bulb
behind an ABS plastic lens.[^brochure]

The controls sit on the left side of the base unit. A volume knob sets the
public-address level, and a keyed microphone socket with a locking ring holds
the microphone plug, which stows on a clip when not in use. The power control is a
momentary rocker switch that returns to its rest position after being pressed, with a
status light beneath it: the light is green when the unit is on, red while the
battery is charging, and flashing or off once the battery is fully
charged.[^manual]

Power comes from an internal 12 volt battery that IST rates at 12 to 14 hours
per charge. Charging is from the supplied 110 V to 28 V AC wall transformer
through a connector near the switch, and the status light shows red until the
battery is full.[^manual][^brochure] IST describes an intelligent charging
circuit that prevents overcharging, so the unit can be left on charge, and says
the battery has no memory effect and need not be run down before recharging; the
manual recommends running on battery power on deck and charging overnight after
each use.[^manual][^brochure] The unit can run while plugged into a GFI outlet,
but IST advises against it because electrical noise can be broadcast through the
speaker.[^manual] Latching straps on the back of the case attach the unit to a
backstroke flagpole, and no-tip feet let it sit directly on the
deck.[^manual][^listing]

## Operation

The starter works the system from the microphone: a bar switch on the side
opens the public address, pressing the small top button while holding the bar
switch sounds the start tone and fires the strobe, and holding the button past
a three second continuous tone sounds the repeating false-start recall
tone.[^manual] The microphone has a 15 foot cord.[^brochure] Public-address
volume is set with the knob on the left side of the base unit, and IST advises
the starter not to stand in front of the speaker to avoid feedback.[^manual] For
start training, the speaker can be turned off so the unit fires the strobe
alone.[^brochure]

The optional SWIMSTART Remote Start is a wireless transmitter and receiver that
lets an official trigger the start tone, the recall, and the public address
without a cable.[^manual][^brochure] It uses 900 MHz radio in the 903.3 to
921.3 MHz band with eight selectable channels, and the receiver responds only to
the transmitter's encoded start and audio signals, which IST says prevents an
accidental start or stray audio.[^brochure] IST rates the range at more than
100 yards and states the radio is FCC approved and needs no operator
license.[^brochure] The handheld transmitter runs on a 9 volt alkaline battery,
with a low-battery warning and an estimated 20 hours of continuous transmission;
the receiver is powered by the SWIMSTART.[^brochure] One transmitter can trigger
receivers on two starts at once, and the package can be added to other
electronic starts.[^brochure]

## Audio

Beyond the case-mounted 40 watt speaker, IST has sold two external speaker
accessories:

- The Far End 40 Watt Speaker (`SPEAKER40W`), a second 40 watt, 120 dB speaker
  that mounts on the backstroke flagpole at the far side of the pool with a
  mounting strap; its connecting cable is sold separately. The current catalog
  lists it as an extra 40 watt speaker with mounting strap.[^farend][^cat40w]
- The [under-block speaker system](../external-speaker/ist.md), a set of 6 watt
  lane speakers on a multi-lane cable, which connected as an accessory; it no
  longer appears in the current catalog.

## Connections

The start output is selectable between normally-open and normally-closed
contacts on a three-hole socket: IST's own timing system, Colorado
Time Systems, and Daktronics timing consoles use the normally-open side, and
Omega timing consoles use the normally-closed side.[^manual] The socket has a
center contact marked "common" for the cable's ground tab, and a rubber plug
fills the unused hole.[^manual] The start cable, part number `SWSTRTCBL-25`, is
sold separately.[^listing]

## Care and maintenance

For off-season storage, IST directs the user to fully charge the battery, then
disconnect it from the wall transformer and wipe water spots from the case with
glass cleaner and a soft cloth. After storage of a month or more, the battery
should be charged again before the unit is used.[^manual]

## Specifications

| | |
|---|---|
| Dimensions | 12 × 8 × 4 in, aluminum with black anodized finish |
| Weight | 23 lb |
| Speaker | 40 W, 120 dB, case-mounted horn, bridged amplifiers |
| Additional speakers | Supports two; far-end `SPEAKER40W` on backstroke flagpole |
| Strobe | 360 degree, 6,000 V xenon bulb behind ABS lens |
| Microphone | 15 ft cord, talk and start/recall switches |
| Start output | Normally-open (IST, CTS, Daktronics) or normally-closed (Omega) |
| Start cable | `SWSTRTCBL-25`, sold separately |
| Volume | Public-address knob on the left of the base unit |
| Status light | Green on, red charging, flashing or off when charged |
| Battery | Internal 12 V, 12 to 14 h per charge |
| Mounting | Deck feet or backstroke flagpole straps |
| Warranty | Five-year limited (two years on batteries) |

The warranty runs five years on the product and two years on the 12 volt
battery; IST replaces the battery at cost for the remaining three years, and
excludes in-deck wiring, deck plates, and wall plates, which carry a separate
warranty.[^manual]
