Research notes, ALGE SM9 start microphone.

Surfaced 2026-11-19 while building out alge-sm8.md.

ALGE-Timing, Startmicrophone SM9 manual, Version E23-11-14 (4 pp., PDF 14 November 2023), filed
under sources/vendors/alge-timing/start-systems/, read in full: turns the report of a 6 mm (indoor)
or 9 mm (outdoor) blank pistol into a timing impulse and is meant to ignore other loud noise near
the start. Plugs by banana plugs into channel C0 of an ALGE timer, which must provide a pull-up;
draws its power over that line, so it has no battery and needs no speech amplifier. Can also go
into the second banana pair of a speech amplifier so the start line carries both. Fixed with a
Velcro strap (or cable tie) to the barrel, or held on a tripod with its head close to the muzzle.
Active-low output, 3 to 10 V DC, max 100 mA; -20 to 60 C; 147 mm long with grommet, 20 mm max
diameter, about 100 g including the 2 m cable. Page headers read "Bedienungsanleitung" though the
text is English.

ALGE product page 4820 (read 2026-11-19): no SV4/SM needed; not suitable for the SU3; accessories
SV4-S, SV5-BT, BANG2; leaflet in English and German (not downloaded); sport listed is athletics.
SV4/SM product page: "For SM9 use SV4-S"; SV4/SM is for the SM8 and older.

Inferred: SM9 succeeds the SM8 (ALGE calls the SM8 old, and the numbering follows); release date not
established, product ID 4820 is recent and the manual is November 2023. Not in the 2017 catalogue
or the 2026 aquatics catalogue.

This article is a stub. The `SM9` is an [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) start
microphone that straps to a blank pistol and plugs straight into a timer's start channel, which also
powers it; ALGE lists it in place of the [SM8](alge-sm8.md).

See the [start-systems overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every start
system.
