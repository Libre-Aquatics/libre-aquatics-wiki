This article is a stub. The SS-1 (`SS-1`) was a Colorado Time Systems portable loudspeaker
start system, advertised in 1991 at $695 with a hand-held microphone, a 40-watt speaker, a
strobe and an internal gel-cell battery, and listed by CTS as a best seller since 1984.

See the [start-systems overview](index.md) for the shared background on electronic starting
devices, and the [SS2 Electronic Start System](ss2.md) for its successor.
