The Championship Start System (part number `SS`) is a deck-wired electronic start
system for swimming made by
[Colorado Time Systems](../../../vendors/colorado-time-systems.md) (CTS).
Unlike the portable [Infinity](infinity-start-system.md) family, the Championship
drives speakers and visual indicators wired into the pool deck: it can feed a
speaker under each starting block, an auxiliary speaker, an underwater speaker,
and the speedlights on relay-judging platforms, all synchronized with its horn
and strobe. CTS lists the product as discontinued and end-of-life; its
successor is the [Championship Elite](championship-elite-start-system.md)
(`SSE`).[^f875][^product]

## Naming and product line

The Championship is the deck-wired, full-featured member of the CTS start line;
the portable [Infinity](infinity-start-system.md) family is the smaller
alternative. Its successor is the
[Championship Elite](championship-elite-start-system.md) (`SSE`), and its
predecessor is the legacy
[Championship Start System (CHAMP-SSM)](champ-ssm.md) generation. For the full
lineup and a side-by-side comparison, see the [start-systems overview](index.md).

### Versions and part numbers

The Championship name has attached to several part numbers over the years, and the
labels differ between the unit's faceplate, the manual, dealer catalogs, and the
CTS website. The unit itself is simply badged "Championship Start System." CTS's
current product page catalogs the system under the part number `SS`.[^product] Its
power-supply compatibility list groups several Championship variants, `SS`,
`SSF`, `WSS`, and `WSSF`, alongside the Infinity (`INF-SSM`/`INF-SSMF`) and two
legacy start systems, `SS-01` and `SS-02`.[^psu] Following the suffix conventions
CTS uses elsewhere in the line, the `F` denotes the 230 V international-power
variant and the `W` prefix denotes a wireless variant (the unit has a
wireless-receiver connector on its front and right panels).[^f875]

The earlier, pre-2005 generation of the unit, badged the same "Championship
Start System" but carrying the designation `CHAMP-SSM`, and likely the same
generation the `SS-01`/`SS-02` legacy numbers refer to, is documented separately
as the [Championship Start System (CHAMP-SSM)](champ-ssm.md).

CTS also sold the Championship as the start system of its Train Right one-lane
training package (`TR-SYS-2`), paired with a relay judging platform, a Pace Clock
Pro, a pushbutton, and a training-size touchpad; with the Championship in the
package, the platform's speedlights flash with the start. The package, now
discontinued, is described in the
[relay judging platform](../relay-judging/cts.md) article.[^f958][^trsys]

## Design and hardware

The start system is a rectangular case about 13 in on a side and 5.75 in deep,
weighing 17.4 lb, with a 360° strobe on top and connectors on three panels. The
case itself contains no built-in speaker: all start and public-address audio
sounds through the externally connected lane, auxiliary, and underwater
speakers, and the left panel carries mounting holes so the auxiliary
compression speaker can bolt onto the unit.[^f875] The front panel carries two
microphone inputs, each with an independent volume control, a green power LED,
a red low-battery LED, and the
auxiliary-speaker volume control. The left panel has the strobe on/off switch,
the auxiliary-speaker connector and mounting holes, the external-power connector,
the unit on/off switch, and three fuses. The right panel carries the connectors
that make the Championship a deck system: a wireless receiver input, a speedlight
output, a lane-speaker output, a timer-start output, an external-strobe
connector, and a 10-pin military-style wallplate connector that bundles the
speedlight, speaker, timer-start, and strobe lines for in-deck
wiring.[^f875]

The system uses two speaker circuits. The main circuit drives up to twelve
6-watt, 45-ohm lane speakers mounted under the starting blocks, plus any
underwater speaker; its start tone is fixed at full volume. The auxiliary
circuit drives one 10-watt, 8-ohm compression speaker, for example on a
backstroke pole or bolted to the side of the unit, and its start-tone volume is
adjustable. Power comes from two internal 12 V gel-cell batteries, and unlike
the Infinity the Championship can operate while charging.[^f875]

## Operation

Each microphone is from CTS's M2 family, with a push-to-talk switch and two start
buttons for either hand. Pressing push-to-talk with a start button fires the
start: a 0.25-second dual-tone blast, a simultaneous strobe flash, a simultaneous
speedlight flash on any relay-judging platforms, and a start pulse to the timer.
Pressing a start button again signals a false-start recall, a repeating dual
tone sounded from all connected speakers, including any underwater speaker. The
microphone volume controls affect only the public-address audio; the start and
recall tones are fixed at full volume except at the auxiliary speaker.[^f875]

Officials and spectators are meant to start their watches from the strobe or the
block speedlights rather than the horn. World Aquatics rules require the starter's
commands and the starting signal to be heard equally and simultaneously at each
block; see the [start-systems overview](index.md) for the reason and the
governing-body rules.[^f875][^wacr] The strobe circuit needs about 30 seconds
between flashes to recharge.[^f875]

## Connections

The timer-start output emits a normally-open (N.O.) pulse and nothing else, which suits
every CTS timer, [System 5](../timers/system-5.md), System 6 and Dolphin among them. A
timer expecting a normally-closed pulse means sending the unit back for factory
modification.[^f875] The Championship interfaces to a CTS timer through a start
jumper cable, models `SJ-50` through `SJ-200` (the number is the cable
length).[^f875] It can be wired on-deck, with lane speakers, speedlights, and the
timer cabled directly to the start system, or in-deck through the wallplate
connector and a facility's deck plates and cable harnesses. The unit mounts on a
tabletop, a wall (with the wall-mount kit), the `TR-3` tripod, or a flagpole
(`START-FPM-2`); CTS specifies not to use a standard camera tripod.[^f875]

## Specifications

| | |
|---|---|
| Dimensions (H × W × D) | 13.25 × 13 × 5.75 in (33.7 × 33 × 14.6 cm) |
| Weight | 17.4 lb (7.9 kg) |
| Power | 110–240 VAC or internal batteries; operates while charging |

| Speedlights | Drives relay-judging-platform speedlights in up to 12 lanes |
| Microphones | Up to 2 wired (M2 family), independent volume |
| Start output | Normally-open pulses; strobe recharge ~30 s |
| Start tone | 0.25 s dual-tone blast with simultaneous strobe/speedlight |

## Part numbers and accessories

- `SS`, Championship Start System; `SSF`, 230 V-charger variant; `WSS` /
  `WSSF`, wireless variants (suffix meanings follow the CTS line convention).[^psu]
- `M-25` / `M-50` / `M-150` / `M-200`, replacement wired microphones (`M2-C15`
  with a coiled 15 ft cable); the number is the cable length in feet.[^f875][^champ-mic]
- `SJ-50` … `SJ-200`, start jumper cable to the timer; the number is the length.[^f875]
- `LS40-6` / `-8` / `-10` / `-12`, lane-speaker cable harness; the number is the
  lane count.[^f875]
- `SL-6` / `-8` / `-10` / `-12`, speedlight cable; the number is the lane count.[^f875]
- `SP-6/45`, 6-watt, 45-ohm under-block lane speaker; `SP-UND`, underwater
  speaker.[^f875]
- `CMP-WP5` / `-WP25` / `-WP50`, start-system-to-wallplate cable, 5, 25, or 50 ft.[^f875]
- `CES-25` / `-50` / `-100` / `-50m`, external-strobe cable; the number is the
  length (ft, or metres for `-50m`).[^f875]
- `R-920-059`, replacement power supply; `R-420-003`, replacement battery;
  `TR-3`, tripod; `START-FPM-2`, flagpole mount.[^psu]
