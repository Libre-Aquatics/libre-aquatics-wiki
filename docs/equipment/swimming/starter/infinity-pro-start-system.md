The Infinity Pro Start System (part number `INF-PRO`) is a portable electronic
start system for swimming made by
[Colorado Time Systems](../../../vendors/colorado-time-systems.md) (CTS).
It is the current entry-level model in the company's start-system line, and the
direct successor to the [Infinity Start System](infinity-start-system.md)
(`INF-SSM`), which reached end of life in 2024. Like its predecessor it packages
a start horn, a strobe, and a wired microphone into one battery-powered unit with
a molded carrying handle; CTS describes it as "the ideal entry-level swimming
start system for all pools."[^datasheet][^product]

## Naming and product line

The Infinity Pro is the portable, entry-level model of the CTS start line and the
current member of the Infinity family; the deck-wired
[Championship](championship-start-system.md) family is the larger alternative. For
the full lineup and a side-by-side comparison, see the
[start-systems overview](index.md).

The Infinity Pro replaced the older
[Infinity Start System](infinity-start-system.md)
(`INF-SSM`); the two are similar in size and role, but the Pro adds adjustable
strobe intensity and start-tone volume, an alternate start tone, both
normally-open and normally-closed start signaling, a 60-minute automatic shut-off,
and a faster-charging battery. The `INF-PRO-F` variant is the same unit supplied
with a 230 V charger for markets outside North America (the base `INF-PRO` ships
with a 115 V charger); this is the same `-F` suffix CTS uses on the Championship
line.[^datasheet][^psu]

## Design and hardware

The unit measures about 14 in long and weighs 11.0 lb, in a corrosion-resistant
enclosure rated IP56. The internal speaker is a 40-watt moisture- and
corrosion-resistant horn speaker that CTS rates at up to 120 dB at 1 m. A
strobe light on top flashes with the horn, and both the strobe intensity and the
start-tone volume are adjustable so the unit can be set for outdoor, indoor, or
office use. The wired microphone has two start buttons (left and right) for
either hand, plus a push-to-talk switch.[^datasheet]

Power comes from an internal gel-cell battery. CTS rates it for more than 12
hours of race-start operation on a charge; it charges to 90% in about four hours
and 100% in about 6.5 hours, and for safety the unit will not operate while
charging, which keeps AC power off the pool deck. An automatic shut-off powers
the unit down after 60 minutes of inactivity to conserve the battery.[^datasheet]

## Operation

The starter presses push-to-talk with either start button to fire the start: a
horn blast with a simultaneous strobe flash and a start pulse to the timer. A
false-start recall function lets the starter alert the competitors.
The unit can sound the standard CTS start tone or an alternate tone, and doubles
as a limited public-address system for announcements.[^datasheet][^product]

The strobe flashes with the horn, and officials and watch timers are meant to
start from the flash rather than the tone. World Aquatics rules require the
starter's commands and the starting signal to be heard equally and
simultaneously at each block; see the [start-systems overview](index.md) for the
reason and the governing-body rules.[^product][^wacr]

## Connections

A start jumper cable (`R-INF-SJxx`) connects the unit's start output to a deck
plate, wall plate, cable harness, or timer. Unlike the older Infinity, which sent
only normally-open pulses, the Infinity Pro can signal a timer with either
normally-open or normally-closed start signaling, so it works with a wider range
of timers without factory modification.[^datasheet] The speaker port outputs a
maximum of 40 W and accepts one external `R-INF-SPxxx` speaker; the manual
directs that only the CTS-approved Infinity external speaker be attached,
because any other type of speaker can cause the start system to fail.[^datasheet]
Integrated attachment points let the unit mount on a tabletop, on the `TR-3`
tripod, or on a flagpole with the `START-FPM-2` mount.[^datasheet]

## Part numbers and accessories

- `INF-PRO`, Infinity Pro Start System, 115 V charger; `INF-PRO-F`, 230 V-charger
  variant.[^datasheet]
- `M2-xx`, Infinity-series microphone; the suffix is the cable length. `M2-C15`
  has a 15 ft coiled cable; straight-cable options are 25, 50, 100, 150, or
  200 ft.[^datasheet][^product]
- `R-INF-SJxx`, start jumper cable to the timer, in 25, 50, 75, 100, or
  165 ft lengths (the suffix is the length).[^datasheet]
- `R-INF-SPxxx`, external 40 W speaker with 125 ft or 200 ft cable.[^datasheet]
- `SMK-1`, kit to mount a speaker on a backstroke pole.[^datasheet]
- `TR-3`, tripod; `R-085-040`, tripod replacement head; `START-FPM-2`,
  flagpole mount.[^datasheet]
