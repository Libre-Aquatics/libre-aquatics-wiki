The Atlantis Swimming Starter System is a portable start system for swimming
made by [Superior Swim Timing](../../../vendors/superior-swim-timing.md) (SST).
SST, founded in 2010 by Pete Santilli,
sells the Atlantis alongside its laptop timing software, SST timer, mobile
scoreboard, and button and cable lines. SST opened pre-orders in March 2019 and
introduced the starter on May 8, 2019.[^sst][^announce][^news] It combines an
internal speaker, a 360 degree LED start strobe, and a microphone in one
battery-powered unit, and is sold directly by SST and through
SwimNerd.[^shop][^swimnerd] SST publishes no wattage or manufacturer
for the internal speaker. This article is a starting point; the
[start-systems overview](index.md) covers the shared background that applies to
every starter.

## Naming and product line

The Atlantis is sold in two configurations that differ only in mounting: the
base unit, and the unit bundled with the quick-connect pole and wall
mount.[^swimnerd][^pricing] The base package ships with the starter unit and its
built-in 360 degree LED strobe, a microphone with a 25 foot cable, and a power
adapter.[^swimnerd]

## Design and hardware

The unit measures 13.5 by 12 by 8 inches and weighs 8.2 lb. It sits on a table,
mounts on a standard photography tripod, or installs permanently on a pole or
wall; the pole and wall mount with quick connect, which allows side-to-side and
up-and-down adjustment, is sold as a variant. The included microphone has a 25
foot cable, and spare microphones are available.[^atlantis][^swimnerd]

SST rates the battery at three to four days of meet operation per charge, with
a battery monitor showing the state of charge; the unit runs with or without
the charger connected, and indicator lights show charging and external power
status. The batteries are rechargeable and replaceable.[^atlantis][^swimnerd]

## Operation

The start strobe is a 360 degree LED unit that SST describes as visible even in
full sun; an optional external strobe connects with an on/off switch and no
cable changes.[^atlantis] The start outputs are compatible with Colorado Time
Systems and Daktronics timing systems, which SST presents as part of a broader
claim of compatibility with all timing systems, and SST sells starter
microphones for those two systems as separate products.[^atlantis][^pricing]

## Audio

Beyond the internal speaker, the Atlantis drives a full set of
[SST lane speakers](../external-speaker/sst.md) through SST speaker harnesses,
sold in six-, eight-, and ten-lane versions.[^atlantis][^pricing]
