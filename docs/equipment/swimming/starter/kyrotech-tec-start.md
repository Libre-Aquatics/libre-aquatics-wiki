This article is a stub. The Tec-Start was an electronic start and public-address system made by
[Kyrotech](../../../vendors/kyrotech.md), with a speaker for each lane and a removable strobe,
that started Kyrotech's [Tec-Timer](../timers/kyrotech-tec-timer.md) or another timer and ran from
a 12 V battery or mains power.

See the [start-systems overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every start
system.
