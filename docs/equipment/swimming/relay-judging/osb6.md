This article is a stub. The OSB6 is an Omega starting block whose top carries a force-operated
contact that times a relay swimmer's take-off, introduced with automatic relay take-off
detection at the 1984 Olympic Games and read by the [OSM6](../timers/osm6.md) console.

See the [relay take-off platforms overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to
every relay take-off device.
