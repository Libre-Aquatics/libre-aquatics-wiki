This article is a stub. The OSB-3 is an Omega starting block whose top moved slightly under a
swimmer's push to sense relay take-offs, used with the [OSM-5](../timers/osm-5.md) at the 1982
Commonwealth Games, where FINA said it had not been asked to approve it.

The [OSB6](osb6.md) followed at the 1984 Olympic Games; see the
[relay take-off platforms overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
relay take-off device.
