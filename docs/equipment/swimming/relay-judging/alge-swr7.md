This article is a stub. The `SWR7` and `SWR7M` are
[ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) sensor plates that give an existing starting
platform relay take-off and reaction timing, the first screwed down and the second held by a
tension belt.

See the [relay take-off platforms overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to
every relay platform.
