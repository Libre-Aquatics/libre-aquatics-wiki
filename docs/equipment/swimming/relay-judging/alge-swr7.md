The SWR7 is a stainless-steel sensor plate made by [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md)
of Lustenau, Austria, that fits on top of an existing starting platform and turns it into a timed
one. Like the sensor in ALGE's own [SO5](../starting-blocks/alge-so5.md) block, it reports the
moment a swimmer leaves the platform, giving relay take-off, reaction and block-off
times.[^cat2017][^cat2026] It comes in two versions with the same sensor: the `SWR7`, screwed down
for a fixed installation, and the `SWR7M`, held by a tension belt so that it can be moved between
blocks.[^cat2026] ALGE also makes custom plates under the name `SWRFx` that fit inside other makers'
blocks.[^cat2026]

See the [relay take-off platforms overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to
every relay platform.

## Role in the timing system

The SWR7 is the option for pools that keep blocks from another maker. ALGE's catalogues present
it as the retrofit counterpart of its own SO5 and So7 blocks, which have the sensor built
in.[^cat2017][^cat2026] One plate goes on each start-end block and plugs into the lane's
[SWT3](../deck-cabling/alge-swt3.md) swim terminal, which has a single socket for either a starting
block or a relay pad. The terminal also takes the lane's touchpad and push buttons and passes the
times to a [TM-SWIM](../timers/alge-tm-swim.md).[^cat2017][^tmswim] In a relay, the take-off time
is compared with the incoming swimmer's touch to show whether the changeover was early.[^cat2026]

The TM-SWIM manual describes the take-off and reaction times as statistical data. ALGE also
claims there that its systems gain the feature through a software upgrade alone.[^tmswim]

## History and naming

This section covers the models and the dates found. ALGE's earlier relay pad was the
[SWR 2](alge-swr2.md), a 500 × 500 × 20 mm stainless plate strapped onto existing blocks and
listed from the SWIM 2000 era until the 2013 swimming catalogue.[^cat2013] The SWR7 does not appear
in that catalogue. It is in the 2017 general catalogue and in an SO5 and SWR7 leaflet of April 2017,
so it was introduced between those dates.[^cat2013][^cat2017][^leaflet17]

The 2017 catalogue and the German leaflet of the same year call the plate "SWR6" in one sentence;
no SWR6 appears anywhere else, and the name looks like an error.[^cat2017] The current product page
titles it "Swim relay pad".[^product]

The custom `SWRFx` plates are built to sit between the top and bottom of a third-party
block.[^cat2026] ALGE's US distributor lists one such plate, the SWRF6 swim relay pad, made for
Myrtha starting blocks.[^usa]

## Design and construction

The plate is stainless steel 1.4404 with a non-slip surface that ALGE designed and describes as
indestructible.[^tmswim][^cat2026] It carries its own footrest, which swimmers can set for the
track start.[^cat2017][^product] The 2020 product page and the current one give the steel as 1.4401
in their facts lists and 1.4404 in their descriptions; the 2020 Spanish leaflet copies the SO5's
"polypropylene and steel" into the SWR7's material line.[^web2020][^product][^leaflet20]

All of ALGE's documents give the plate's size as 740 × 560 × 40 mm.[^tmswim][^cat2017][^cat2026]
They disagree on its weight:

| Source | Weight |
|---|---|
| 2017 general catalogue | 26 kg |
| 2017 English leaflet | 26 kg |
| 2020 Spanish leaflet | 26 kg |
| 2020 product page | about 24 kg |
| TM-SWIM manual (2024) | 20 kg |
| 2026 aquatics catalogue | 26 kg |

The 740 × 560 mm footprint is the same figure ALGE quotes for its complete SO4 and SO5
blocks.[^cat2013][^cat2026]

## Sensing and calibration

The sensor is calibrated in the same way as the SO5's. Its trigger point should lie between 8 and
15 kg: with the timer disconnected and a buzzer on the output, the plate is pulled back with a
spring balance until the buzzer sounds, and two adjusting screws are reset if it falls outside that
range.[^tmswim] See the [SO5 article](../starting-blocks/alge-so5.md#relay-take-off-sensing) for the
full procedure.

## Installation and mounting

The `SWR7` is screwed to the existing block for a permanent installation; the `SWR7M` is fastened
with a tension belt and can be fitted to or taken off a block at any time.[^cat2026] ALGE says both
fit most starting blocks.[^cat2017][^product] Its documents disagree on whether tools are needed:
the TM-SWIM manual says the plate goes on without tools, and the 2020 product page says no screws or
preparation of the block are needed, which describes the strap model; the same page and the
catalogues give screws as the SWR7's fixing.[^tmswim][^web2020][^cat2026]

## Connection to the timer

The plate's sensor connects to the lane's SWT3, whose single block socket takes either an ALGE
starting block or a relay pad.[^cat2017] ALGE lists the SWT3, its touchpads, its SO starting blocks
and the [SPA2](../external-speaker/alge-spa2-spp2.md) lane speaker as accessories.[^product]

## Care and maintenance

The TM-SWIM manual refers the SWR7's care to its touchpad section: protect the contacts with a
little silicone grease, clean the steel regularly with tap water, keep iron and concrete residue
off it, and avoid dosing chemicals near it.[^tmswim]
