The Colorado Time Systems RJP (relay judging platform) is a family of starting-block
platforms made by
[Colorado Time Systems](../../../vendors/colorado-time-systems.md) (CTS) that
sense the instant a relay swimmer's feet leave the block. The platform reports the
departure to the CTS [timing console](../timers/index.md), which compares it with the
incoming swimmer's [touchpad](../touchpad/index.md) finish to judge the relay exchange,
and with the start impulse to measure start reaction time, both to 1/100 of a
second.[^f507][^ctsrjp] The current line is the RJPLD, which the user guide expands as
relay judging platforms with speedlights and dual connectors.[^f968] Shared background
on how relay exchanges are judged and ruled on is in the
[relay take-off platforms overview](index.md).

## History and generations

CTS documented the original RJP line in a 2004 installation guide, which lists three
styles: the Classic RJP, the Lighted RJP with Speedlight LEDs across the front edge, and
the Colorado Block, a combination of Paragon Aquatics' Track Start Competitor starting
block with a CTS Lighted RJP, an under-block speaker, and a connection hub.[^f507] The
guide describes stainless steel construction and states that the platforms sense
departures accurately for swimmers weighing at least 90 pounds (40.8 kg).[^f507]

The RJPLD generation replaces the single rear terminal block with identical connector
blocks on both sides of the platform and is built from aluminum.[^f968][^ctsrjp] CTS
mounting documents show RJPLD part numbers in production by 2010.[^f955] A 2024 service
sheet addresses platforms designated RJP, RJPL, and RJPLD, indicating the earlier
designations remained in service.[^f1047]

## Operation

Both the top and the leading edge of the platform are sensitive to the swimmer's
departure, and CTS states the design distinguishes weight shifts from an actual
take-off.[^f507][^f968] On each start the platform supplies the departure time to the
timer, which compares it with the start impulse to produce a start reaction time;
System 6 and later consoles can post reaction times to the scoreboard.[^f968] In relays
the timer compares the departure with the incoming swimmer's touchpad finish and prints
the exchange result with the race data.[^f968]

The platform measures the force the swimmer exerts on the block. CTS states that, based
on sensor data and high-speed camera analysis, a swimmer may still be in contact with
the block while exerting less force than the platform can detect, that this forceless
contact was never observed to last longer than 0.01 seconds, and that its Swimming
software therefore adds a fixed, non-adjustable 0.01 seconds in favor of a safe take-off
to every relay exchange time on all CTS timers.[^f968] This is the manufacturer-defined
differential that governing-body rules defer to; CTS notes that other companies'
hardware and software handle the starting point in various ways and recommends using CTS
platforms with CTS timers.[^f968] The World Aquatics facilities rules likewise state
that the manufacturer of the device shall be consulted for the relay take-off
differential.[^wa-fr]

## Speedlights and start indicators

Lighted platforms carry a row of LEDs, which CTS brands Speedlights, across the front
edge: twenty high-intensity calibrated LEDs on the current RJPLD.[^ctsrjp] The LEDs
flash simultaneously with the start tone and the start strobe so that swimmers can see
the start without changing their stance; CTS presents this as providing an equal start
for athletes who are deaf or hard of hearing.[^f507][^f968] USA Swimming's rules for
deaf and hard-of-hearing swimmers likewise provide for visual starting
signals.[^usaswim] The RJPLD adds a deckside start indicator: an LED set into the
deck-facing side of the connector block, flashing with the start to cue backup
timers.[^f968]

Flashing with the start signal requires a link from the Speedlights to a CTS
Championship-series start system, made through a connection hub beneath the block, a deck
plate, or a cable harness; the 2019 user guide names the Championship Start system and the current
product page names the Championship Elite. If they are not connected, the platform still
judges exchanges but the lights do not flash.[^f968][^ctsrjp]

The current CTS datasheet describes the Speedlight technology as patented, and the
product page cites US patent D464,699.[^rjpds][^ctsrjp] Patent records show that number is a
design patent titled "Swimmer's starting block", filed in 2000 by Keith M. Brice and
granted in 2002, covering the ornamental design of a starting block with integrated
strobe lighting; it is now assigned to PlayCore Wisconsin, part of CTS's parent company
PlayCore, and is not a utility patent on the platform sensor or its LEDs.[^brice]

Several sizes match specific starting blocks. The RJPLD-2016 straps onto Omega OSB11 and
OSB12 blocks, and an unlighted RJPD-2016 variant is documented alongside it.[^f955] The
RJPLD-OSB12 bolts onto OSB12 track-start blocks through two 7 mm holes drilled at the
rear of the block, secured with M6 hardware.[^f975][^f976] An RJPLD-SL-2012 hooks over
the front edge of Steel Line track-start blocks.[^f1012] The platforms are compatible
with most starting-block manufacturers, and custom top logos are available.[^ctsrjp]

## Mounting and connection

Most platforms strap to the starting block: two strap assemblies loop under the block to
a hook and buckle, are adjusted once to fit the block, and are then attached and
tensioned each time the platform is placed.[^f968][^f1047] Platforms for some blocks
instead have stainless steel bolts welded to the underside that drop into pre-drilled
holes and are secured with nuts, and a few block models ship with custom mounting
instructions.[^f507][^f968] CTS advises against leaving a platform strapped to the block
when not in use.[^f963]

The RJPLD's two connector blocks are identical, and the cable plugs into whichever side
poses the least trip hazard; CTS states the connector runs parallel to the platform to
reduce the risk of accidental disconnection as swimmers step up to the
block.[^f968][^ctsrjp] Two cables serve the current line: the R-015-027, two cables
joined together, whose banana-plug leg stacks into the Button A/RJP jack of a DP-series
deck plate, connection hub, or cable harness and whose mini BNC leg carries the
Speedlight circuit, and the R-015-046, a triple banana plug for TDP-series deck
plates.[^f968][^f958] The legacy RJP instead used a rear terminal block with a
dual-banana RJP cable and a separate Speedlight cable ending in a 2-pin DIN
connector.[^f507]

On the console side, the timer is set to accept platform rather than button input on the
lane and to print relay exchange results. The legacy guide adds a version floor for the
[System 5](../timers/system-5.md): its Swimming software must be at 3.29.20 or newer
before the RJP will work.[^f507][^f968]

## Train Right package

CTS also sold the RJPLD as the centerpiece of the Train Right package (TR-SYS), a
standalone, portable one-lane training system that pairs the platform with a Pace Clock
Pro, a pushbutton, a training-size touchpad, and a start system.[^tr][^trsys] The pace
clock runs the training modes and displays relay exchange, start reaction, turn speed,
and breakout times.[^tr][^f957] Two versions were cataloged: TR-SYS-1 with the Infinity
Start System and TR-SYS-2 with the Championship Start System, each with the TP-30
touchpad (30 × 22 in); 220-volt versions, listed as /F or -220, substitute the TP-76X90
touchpad (76 × 90 cm).[^tr][^f957]

In the package the platform plugs into the pace clock's Relay Platform jack with the
same R-015-027 cable used at competition. With the Championship Start System the
cable's mini BNC leg plugs into the starter's Speedlights jack, with an optional
R-015-593 extension cable between the platform and the starter; with the Infinity Start
System the mini BNC leg is left coiled, because the Speedlights do not activate with
that starter.[^f957][^f958][^tr] The current CTS site lists the package as
discontinued.[^trsys]

## Care and maintenance

CTS's connector maintenance routine applies: dielectric grease in each banana jack
before use, cleaning with isopropyl alcohol after use, and a diluted calcium-lime-rust
cleaner only as a last resort, never on Speedlight connectors.[^f968]

## Part numbers and accessories

- CAD-RJPL-1 and CAD-RJPL-2, wheeled aluminum caddies for 20 in and 24 in wide platforms
  respectively; each stores and transports up to ten platforms and weighs 60 lb
  (27 kg).[^caddy]
- R-SL-xx Speedlight cable harnesses in 6-, 8-, 10-, and 12-lane versions, the
  alternative to under-block connection hubs for wiring the Speedlights.[^ctsrjp][^f507]
- RJPLD tester, a 9-volt battery-powered box that plugs into either connector block and
  runs a take-off test and two Speedlight tests before a session.[^f969]
