This article is a stub. The `023-02` is the [ALGE-Timing](../../../../vendors/alge-timing.md)
waterproof push button for hand timing, on a 2 m cable with a banana plug, and the `023-10` is
the same button on a 10 m cable.

See the [pushbuttons overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
pushbutton.
