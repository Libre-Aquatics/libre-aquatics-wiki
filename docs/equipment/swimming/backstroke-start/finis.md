The Backstroke Start Wedge made by [FINIS](../../../vendors/finis.md) is a
[backstroke start device](index.md): a foot wedge that hangs from a starting block on
two straps and gives a backstroke swimmer a non-slip foothold on the wall at the start
of a race. The swimmer sets the wedge height by moving a hook between five numbered
settings on the straps. It is a manual device with no retraction mechanism, in contrast
to self-retracting designs such as the
[CTS Backstroke Start Device](cts.md).[^manual][^sw2018] FINIS introduced the wedge in
March 2018, developed in collaboration with Vince Harris of WEDG Swim Start Systems
(County Cork, Ireland), an engineer who had begun building wedges for his daughter's
swim team after finding existing devices expensive.[^sw2018][^product]

FINIS markets the wedge on cost: the company describes it as about one-fifth the price
of automatic wedge systems, with setup in under two minutes, and states that it is
FINA-compliant under rule FR 2.10 and usable in both training and
competition.[^sw2018][^faq] That rule number comes from an older edition of the
rulebook; in the 2021–2025 FINA Facilities Rules the backstroke ledge specification is
FR 2.1.11.[^fr] An independent reviewer in 2018 judged the price (then US$249.99, listed
at US$270 as of 2026) high in absolute terms but well below other devices.[^wizard] The
regulatory status of ledges by governing body, the history of the equipment, and the
foot-placement rule that governs its use are covered in the
[backstroke start devices overview](index.md).

## Design and components

The wedge itself is an injection-molded thermoplastic rubber (TPR) bar finished with a
self-adhesive grip tape for foot placement.[^faq] Internal hardware comprises rods of
316 stainless steel and a Q235 steel insert plate.[^techsheet] The wedge hangs from two
wedge straps threaded through its body, each ending in a Z hook; two numbered block
straps loop around the block and receive the Z hooks at one of five settings. An
optional S hook attaches the assembly to a block edge instead, and a keeper secures
excess strap. The wedge has inside and outside strap routings: the inside routing suits
attachment to a block's hand grips and the outside routing suits attachment to its
posts.[^manual]

The device has no powered or spring-driven parts. The reviewer noted the simple
construction as a durability advantage, since there is little that can
malfunction.[^wizard]

## Installation and mounting

The block straps loop around whatever structure the starting block offers: the single
post of a single-post block, both posts of a two-post block, the back two posts of a
four-post block, or the backstroke hand grips. On a large-base block with no accessible
posts, the S hook attaches to the block's edge instead.[^manual]

To set the wedge up, the straps are attached to the block, the Z hooks are secured at
the 0 setting, and the wedge strap length is adjusted until the top of the wedge sits at
water level.[^manual] The straps stretch slightly the first time the assembly is wetted
and are stable afterward, so the initial setting is best checked in the water.[^wizard]

## Operation

The wedge is made to be adjusted by the swimmer. From the baseline setup, the swimmer
moves each Z hook among the five settings, numbered −4 to +4, to raise or lower the
wedge to preference; the 0 setting places the top of the wedge at water
level.[^manual] This range corresponds to the World Aquatics facilities rule, which
allows a backstroke ledge to be adjusted 4 cm above or below the water
level.[^fr] After the start the wedge stays on the wall; it has no retraction mechanism
and is repositioned or removed by hand.[^manual][^sw2018]

## Care and maintenance

FINIS's care guidance centers on the grip tape, whose adhesive can delaminate with
regular use and with large temperature swings. The company advises checking the tape
for peeling before first use and reactivating the adhesive with a heat gun or hair
dryer if needed, sealing the tape edges with a silicone caulk rated for plastic, not
leaving the wedge in the pool, and storing it in a cool, dry place. Peeling tape can be
replaced: the old tape is removed, the surface cleaned with isopropyl alcohol, and the
replacement tape applied with heat and pressure.[^faq] FINIS sells the grip tape,
straps, and hooks as replacement parts.[^techsheet]
