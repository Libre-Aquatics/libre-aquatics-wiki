The BSA (Backstroke Start Aid) is a removable backstroke start ledge sold by
[ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) of Lustenau, Austria. It hooks over the top of a
starting platform and hangs down the end wall on two straps, giving a backstroke swimmer a footrest
at the water line to push off from.[^manual][^cat2026] The swimmer can set the footrest height from
the water by turning a sprung adjustment ring, and officials lift the whole unit off by its handlebar
after the start.[^manual] ALGE says it fits its own SO4 and SO5 blocks and any other make of
platform.[^manual][^tmswim] The earliest ALGE document found that lists it is the 2017 general
catalogue.[^cat2017]

See the [backstroke start devices overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to
every backstroke start device, including the World Aquatics rules on backstroke ledges.

## Role in the timing system

The BSA is purely mechanical: its manual describes no sensor or cable. It sits on the start-end
block alongside ALGE's timing equipment: the [SO5](../starting-blocks/alge-so5.md) or
[SO4](../starting-blocks/alge-so4.md) block, or the [SWR7](../relay-judging/alge-swr7.md) relay pad
on another maker's block.[^product][^manual] Because the footrest hangs in front of the end wall, the
manual directs officials such as the turn inspectors to remove each unit as soon as the start has
gone, so that the wall and any [touchpad](../touchpad/index.md) are clear for the turns.[^manual]

## Design and components

This section lists the parts the manual describes. The unit has two halves. The lower half is the
footrest, which rests against the end wall. The upper half hooks onto the platform and carries the
adjustment mechanism, joined to the footrest by two straps.[^manual]

| Part | Material and role |
|---|---|
| Footrest | Injection-moulded, glass-fibre-reinforced polypropylene; dense enough not to float; dotted anti-slip surface |
| Straps | Woven high-tenacity polyester, made without stretch |
| Rims | UV-resistant ABS; the straps wind onto them, also for storage |
| Bars | Composite tubes of 30 mm and 40 mm diameter; the upper one is the handlebar |
| Supports | UV-resistant ABS, with a fin that fits ALGE and other platforms; the left one carries the adjustment ring |
| Pads | Soft PVC, glued under the supports to protect the platform |
| Adjustment ring | ABS, with a numbered scale on which 0 is the water level |
| Spring | AISI 302 stainless-steel wire, returning the mechanism to position |
| Locking ring | ABS, holding the spring on the 40 mm bar |

Source: BSA manual.[^manual]

## Adjustment and use

The unit comes assembled. The only setting made for a particular pool is the height of the
footrest: with the adjustment ring's three screws loosened, the straps are wound until the top
edge of the footrest is level with the water, and the ring is locked again with 0 on its scale at
the arrow. The manual limits those screws to 3 N·m.[^manual] From that zero point each swimmer turns
the ring to set the footrest for their own start position and size; the return spring lets this be
done with one hand from the water.[^manual][^tmswim] The TM-SWIM manual describes the adjustment as
2 cm steps up or down.[^tmswim]

Before a race the manual asks for the straps to pass under the rims on the rear side, to be
pulled taut, and for the footrest bar to hang level. The footrest must be backed by the wall
throughout the start, and the ledge must never be loaded when it is turned to 90°.[^manual] Because
the ledge lengthens the start, the manual advises swimmers to practise with it before using it in
competition and to check that no one is behind them.[^manual] It leaves the referees responsible
for checking that each installation meets the competition rules.[^manual]

## Installation and mounting

ALGE describes the mounting as universal and needing no tools.[^cat2026] The manual adds a fixing
point: the BSA engages a screw on the platform's top. ALGE's SO4 and SO5 and many other blocks
already have one; otherwise the supplied round blocking units, each a screw and a plastic part, are
fitted into prepared holes, or holes are drilled 230 mm back from the block's front edge. The
blocking unit must not stand above the top surface of the block.[^manual]

## Care and maintenance

The BSA manual asks for the unit to be rinsed in fresh water whenever it has been used and dried before
storage, for the stainless screws and spring to be checked for rust, especially after salt water,
and for cleaning with nothing stronger than mild detergent or soapy water. It warns against hypochlorite,
chloride cleaners, strong alcohol and solvents such as acetone, against storing heavy objects on
the unit, and against using it after a hard drop without checking it.[^manual] The TM-SWIM manual's
shorter entry says only to wipe it with a dry cloth.[^tmswim] ALGE sells a wheeled stainless-steel
stand, the [CADDY BSA](../accessories/alge-caddy-bsa.md), for storing and moving the units.[^product][^caddy]

The two sets of dimensions differ widely. The 786 mm and 150 mm figures appear in both the 2021
product page and the 2026 catalogue, with the middle figure given as the maximum strap length;
the TM-SWIM manual gives 900 × 200 × 150 mm instead, and ALGE does not explain the
difference.[^web2021][^cat2026][^tmswim]
