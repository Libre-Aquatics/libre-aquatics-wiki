This article is a stub. The `KT150`, `KT300` and `KT500` are
[ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) reels of 150, 300 and 500 m of two-wire field
telephone line, used to extend a start line or the data line to a
[GAZ4](../../common/scoreboard/alge-gaz4.md) display board.

See the [timers overview](../timers/index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console.
