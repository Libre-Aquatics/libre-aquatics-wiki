This article is a stub. The `NGL13` is the [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) mains
charger named in the [STB1 Startbeep](../starter/alge-stb1.md) manual, which plugs into the STB1's
6-pin DIN socket to recharge a 9 V NiCd cell.

See the [timers overview](../timers/index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console.
