This article is a stub. The `MC2-S` is an [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) adapter
that connects a touchpad and a starting platform for each of two lanes to a
[Timy3](../timers/alge-timy.md) through its 25-pin socket, for timing swim training.

See the [deck cabling overview](../deck-cabling/index.md) for the shared background on lane wiring, and the
[MC8 push-button cable](../deck-cabling/alge-mc8.md) for its eight-lane counterpart.
