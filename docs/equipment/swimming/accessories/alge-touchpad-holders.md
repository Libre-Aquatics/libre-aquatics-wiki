This article is a stub. The `TP-D140`, `TP-S` and `TP-T` are
[ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) stainless-steel holders that clamp a
[TP touchpad](../touchpad/alge-tp-touchpads.md) to the pool wall or deck with an M8 screw.

See the [touchpads overview](../touchpad/index.md) for the shared background that applies to every touchpad.
