This article is a stub. The `SV4` is an [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md)
battery-powered speech amplifier that connects a starter's headset to the timing operator over a
two-wire line; the `SV4/SM` version also powers an [SM8](alge-sm8.md) start microphone
and passes its impulse on the same line.

See the [timers overview](../timers/index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console.
