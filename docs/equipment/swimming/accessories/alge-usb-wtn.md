This article is a stub. The `USB-WTN` is an [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md)
adapter that ALGE first made to connect a [WTN](alge-wtn.md) radio unit to a PC over USB, and that
now links several ALGE timers and display boards to a PC.

See the [timers overview](../timers/index.md) for the shared background on timing consoles.
