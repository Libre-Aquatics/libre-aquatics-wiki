The smaller items used with swim timing equipment: printers, power supplies, headsets,
start microphones, wireless links, cables, service tools, holders and transport cases.

## What lives here

- Printers, power supplies and chargers for timers.
- Headsets, speech amplifiers, start microphones and start lights used with start systems.
- Wireless links and interface units.
- Separate cables and leads, apart from the deck wiring itself.
- Test meters, pumps, simulators and programming tools used for service.
- Holders for touchpads, and bags, boxes, stands and trolleys for transport and storage.

The harnesses, plates and lane modules on the deck have their own section,
[deck cabling](../deck-cabling/index.md), and loudspeakers are under
[external speakers](../external-speaker/index.md). Items that serve
scoreboards, pace clocks or more than one discipline are under
[common cables and accessories](../../common/accessories/index.md).
