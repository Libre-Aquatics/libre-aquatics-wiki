The SO4 and So7 are stainless-steel starting platforms made by
[ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) of Lustenau, Austria. Each has a five-position
footrest for the track start and an integrated sensor that reports relay take-off, reaction and
block-off times.[^cat2013][^cat2026] The SO4 appears in ALGE's 2013 swimming catalogue; the So7
appears in its 2026 aquatics catalogue, which no longer lists the SO4.[^cat2013][^cat2026] Both
were sold beside ALGE's polyethylene [SO5](alge-so5.md) as the steel alternative.[^cat2013][^cat2026]

See the [starting blocks overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
starting block, including the World Aquatics rules on platform height, size and slope.

## Role in the timing system

The SO4 and So7 are start-end blocks for an ALGE automatic timing installation, one per lane. Their
sensor connects to the lane's [SWT3](../deck-cabling/alge-swt3.md) swim terminal, which also takes the
lane's touchpad and push buttons and passes the times to a
[TM-SWIM](../timers/alge-tm-swim.md).[^cat2026][^tmswim] The TM-SWIM manual allows the SWT3 to be
placed either behind the lane or inside an SO4 or SO5 block.[^tmswim] At a relay changeover, the
time the swimmer leaves the plate is compared with the incoming swimmer's touch to check for an
early take-off.[^cat2026]

ALGE's current product page groups its blocks as two kinds, stainless steel and plastic, alongside
the [SWR7](../relay-judging/alge-swr7.md) relay pad for other makers' blocks.[^product]

## History and naming

This section covers the models and the dates found. The SO4 is in ALGE's swimming catalogue of
September 2013, described as stainless steel 1.4571 with a footrest that can be removed without
tools, and with other sizes and designs available on request.[^cat2013] ALGE's 2017 general
catalogue lists only the SO5 among its blocks.[^cat2017] The TM-SWIM manual of 2024 still describes
the SO4, and ALGE's US distributor still lists it in 2026.[^tmswim][^usa]

The So7 appears in the 2026 aquatics catalogue, which lists it beside the SO5 and drops the
SO4.[^cat2026] ALGE does not say whether the So7 replaces the SO4 or is a new design. The current
product page shows an image it describes as an So7 but names no model in its text.[^product] The
TM-SWIM manual also mentions an SO3 once, among blocks the backstroke start aid fits; no other
document names it.[^tmswim]

## Design and construction

This section covers the two models' frames and footrests. Both blocks are stainless steel. ALGE
gives the SO4's steel as 1.4571 in 2013, while the TM-SWIM manual prints "1.44004", which is not a
standard grade and is presumably a misprint.[^cat2013][^tmswim] The So7 is steel
1.4404.[^cat2026] Both have a footrest with five settings that comes off without tools, so the block
can stay in place for public swimming.[^cat2013][^cat2026] The 2026 catalogue credits the So7's sensor
with measuring reaction time and jump duration, as it does the SO5's.[^cat2026]

| | SO4 | So7 |
|---|---|---|
| Material | Stainless steel 1.4571 | Stainless steel 1.4404 |
| Footrest | 5 positions, removable | 5 stages, removable |
| Dimensions | 740 × 560 × 400 mm | 560 × 740 × 645 mm |
| Weight | 40 kg | 42 kg |
| First document | September 2013 | June 2026 |

Sources: 2013 and 2026 catalogues and TM-SWIM manual.[^cat2013][^cat2026][^tmswim]

The SO4's figure of 740 × 560 × 400 mm is the one ALGE also prints for the SO5 and, with a 40 mm
height, for the SWR7 relay pad. For the SO5, ALGE's own dimension drawing contradicts it, showing that block about 610 mm
high.[^cat2013][^tmswim] The TM-SWIM manual refers the
SO4's dimensions to "the drawing on the next page", but the drawing there is the SO5's.[^tmswim]

## Relay take-off sensing

The SO4's sensor is calibrated in the same way as the SO5's, to trigger at a pull-back of 8 to
15 kg; see the [SO5 article](alge-so5.md#relay-take-off-sensing) for the procedure.[^tmswim]
The top plate is held by four nuts, marked with labels, at the front and back. They are loosened
before a competition so that the plate can move, and tightened to 17 N·m for public use. A photo in
the manual shows two of them on the SO4's steel frame.[^tmswim][^nuts]

## Installation and connection

ALGE's documents do not describe how the SO4 or So7 is fixed to the deck. The sensor connects to the
lane's SWT3, which can sit inside an SO4.[^tmswim] ALGE lists the SWT3, the
[BSA](../backstroke-start/alge-bsa.md) backstroke start aid and the
[SPA2](../external-speaker/alge-spa2-spp2.md) lane speaker as accessories for its blocks.[^product]

## Care and maintenance

ALGE's instructions for the SO4 call for occasional cleaning with a mild cleaner, followed by a
rinse with fresh water, to prevent corrosion.[^nuts][^tmswim]

## Compared with the SO5

The steel blocks are heavier than the SO5 (40–42 kg against 24 kg in current documents) and have
five footrest positions against the SO5's six.[^cat2013][^cat2026] All three report reaction and relay
take-off times, and the manual calibrates the SO4 and SO5 the same way.[^cat2026][^tmswim] The 2026 catalogue describes the
So7 as having an "exclusive design", a phrase it does not use for the SO5.[^cat2026]
