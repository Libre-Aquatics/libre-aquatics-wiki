This article is a stub. The `SO4` and `So7` are [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md)
stainless-steel starting platforms with an adjustable footrest and integrated sensor, with the
`So7` appearing in the 2026 catalogue in place of the `SO4`.

See the [starting blocks overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
starting block.
