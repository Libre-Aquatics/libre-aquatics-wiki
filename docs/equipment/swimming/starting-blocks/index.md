This article is a stub. A starting block, or starting platform, is the raised platform at the end
of each lane that swimmers dive from, and several are sold with timing equipment built in, such as
a relay take-off platform, lane speaker or connection hub.

See the [relay take-off platforms overview](../relay-judging/index.md) for the timing equipment
that mounts on a block.
