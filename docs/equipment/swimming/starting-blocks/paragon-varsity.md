This article is a stub. Varsity is a [Paragon Aquatics](../../../vendors/paragon-aquatics.md)
series of low-height starting platforms with a 24 × 20 in top, introduced in 1990 for a revised
high-school rule that set an 18 in height above the water at some pools.

See the [starting blocks overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
starting block.
