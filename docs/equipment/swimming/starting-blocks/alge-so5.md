The SO5 is a starting platform made by [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) of
Lustenau, Austria, from polyethylene with stainless-steel reinforcement.[^cat2026] It has a
footrest for the track start that can be set in six positions or removed, and a sensing top plate
that reports the instant the swimmer leaves it, giving reaction times at the start and relay
take-off times at each changeover.[^cat2017][^manual] ALGE sells it as part of its
[TM-SWIM](../timers/alge-tm-swim.md) swimming system, built for permanent installation indoors or
outdoors.[^cat2026] The earliest document found that names it is ALGE's swimming catalogue of
September 2013.[^cat2013]

See the [starting blocks overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
starting block, including the World Aquatics rules on platform height, size and slope.

## Role in the timing system

The SO5 is the start-end platform of an ALGE automatic timing installation, one per lane. Its
sensor is wired to the lane's [SWT3](../deck-cabling/alge-swt3.md) swim terminal, which also takes
the lane's touchpad and push buttons and passes everything to the TM-SWIM.[^cat2026][^product] At
the start, the moment the swimmer leaves the plate gives the reaction time. In a relay, comparing
that moment against the touch of the incoming swimmer on the [touchpad](../touchpad/index.md) shows whether
the take-off was early.[^leaflet17][^cat2017] ALGE's catalogues also list a block-off time, and
since about 2017 a jump duration measured with the SWT3.[^cat2017][^cat2026]

The SO5 replaces the whole block. Pools that keep their existing blocks can add the [SWR7](../relay-judging/alge-swr7.md) or SWR7M relay take-off plate instead,
which ALGE describes as the retrofit option; the two share a leaflet.[^leaflet17][^cat2026]

## Design and construction

This section covers the body, top plate and footrest. The body is a moulded polyethylene shell. The
2013 catalogue and the 2017 leaflet call it LDPE; the 2017 catalogue and the 2026 catalogue add
stainless-steel reinforcement; and a 2020 Spanish leaflet describes it as polypropylene and
steel.[^cat2013][^leaflet17][^cat2017][^cat2026][^leaflet20] The 2017 catalogue also announced a new
top surface for better grip.[^cat2017]

ALGE's dimension drawing of November 2021 shows the block about 751 mm long and 600 mm wide, with
the front edge of the top plate 610 mm above the base. The plate falls 8° toward the pool, and the
footrest at the rear is set at 30°. A tubular bar runs across the front of the block.[^manual] The
footrest locks into six positions and can be taken off without tools, so the block can stay in
place for public swimming.[^cat2013][^cat2017]

drawing and the product page agree with each other and not with that figure.[^manual][^product] The drop from 35 kg to 24 kg coincides with the 2017 catalogue's
announcement of steel reinforcement and a new surface; whether the block was redesigned then is not
stated.[^cat2017]

## Relay take-off sensing

The top plate rests on the sensor, whose trigger point is set between 8 and 15 kg. To check it, the timing system is disconnected, a buzzer is connected to the
block's output, and the top plate is pulled back with a spring balance until the buzzer
sounds.[^manual] If the reading falls outside the range, two adjusting screws under the plate are
reset in turn: each is screwed in until the buzzer sounds, backed off about half a turn and locked,
and the test is repeated.[^manual]

The plate is held by four nuts, marked with labels, at the front and back. Before a competition
they must be loosened so that the plate can move; for public use they are tightened to 17 N·m.
The block comes with a torque wrench for this.[^manual][^nuts][^product]

## Installation and mounting

The block has two mounting plates with slotted holes for fixing it to the deck.[^manual] ALGE's
drawing requires its front edge to be flush with the touchpad hanging on the wall, so a block on a
wall without a touchpad overhangs the pool edge by 10 mm.[^manual] For permanent wiring, ALGE can
supply the block with a hole in the base so that the cable conduit enters it from below.[^manual]

## Connection to the timer

The block's sensor output connects to the lane's SWT3 with a banana cable, which is part of the
delivery.[^product] ALGE lists the SWT3, the [BSA](../backstroke-start/alge-bsa.md) backstroke start
aid and its [SPA2](../external-speaker/alge-spa2-spp2.md) lane speaker as accessories.[^product]
The 2026 catalogue's system drawing places an SO5, a touchpad, push buttons and an SWT3 at each
lane.[^cat2026]

## Care and maintenance

ALGE recommends occasional cleaning of the block with a mild cleaner, followed by a rinse with fresh
water, to avoid corrosion.[^manual][^nuts] The sensor's trigger point should be checked
with the plate's nuts loosened.[^manual]

## Compared with the SO4 and So7

ALGE has sold stainless-steel blocks alongside the SO5. In 2013 the [SO4](alge-so4.md), in steel
1.4571, had a five-position footrest and weighed 40 kg; in 2026 the So7, in steel 1.4404, also has
five positions and weighs 42 kg. The SO5 is the lighter plastic block with six footrest
positions.[^cat2013][^cat2026] All three report reaction and relay take-off times.[^cat2013][^cat2026]
The earlier [SO 2](alge-so2.md) was the block of the SWIM 2000 system.[^arch]
