The Colorado Block is a starting block made jointly by [Paragon Aquatics](../../../vendors/paragon-aquatics.md)
and [Colorado Time Systems](../../../vendors/colorado-time-systems.md) (CTS) and announced in March 2002.
It is a Paragon starting platform fitted with CTS's
[relay judging platform](../relay-judging/cts.md) (RJP) and speedlights, a start speaker under the
top, and a connection hub that moves the timing connections from the deck to the underside of the
block.[^pr2002][^arch] The original base was Paragon's
[Track Start Competitor](track-start-competitor.md), and Paragon also offers the same fittings
for several of its other platforms.[^pr2002][^paragon] The two companies registered a joint
design patent for the block in 2003.[^patent]

## Role in the timing system

This section places the block among CTS timing equipment; the general background is on the
[starting blocks overview](index.md) and the [relay take-off platforms overview](../relay-judging/index.md).

The RJP on top detects the moment the swimmer's feet come off it. The timer compares that moment with
the start signal for a reaction time or a false-start check, or, in a relay, with the touch of the
swimmer coming in.[^sw0204][^pr2002] LED speedlights on the RJP flash at the same moment as the horn and
strobe, so a swimmer who cannot hear the horn can see the start.[^pr2002] The start speaker under
the top plays the start tone at the lane.[^arch][^cat11] The connection hub gives the RJP, its
speedlights and the backup buttons a place to plug in.[^f968]

CTS promotes the block for three things: timing accuracy in competition, training starts and
relay exchanges against measured results, and fewer cables on the deck.[^cat15] Those are CTS's
claims for its RJPs and its QuickConnect cabling rather than independent findings.

## History

This section covers the launch and the block's use.

CTS and Paragon, at the time a Pentair Pool Products division, announced the Colorado Block in a joint
press release on 4 March 2002. The release described a Paragon Track Start Side Mount Competitor
built with a CTS RJP, to be sold through Paragon's distributors.[^pr2002] CTS's chief executive,
Anita Sayed, said in it that CTS could have designed its own block but preferred to work with
Paragon and keep to timing. Paragon's president, Tom Saldarelli, called the result a single
starting block from the two companies.[^pr2002] Swimming World reported the launch in April 2002,
and a joint advertisement in July 2002 pointed readers to the block's own website,
coloradoblock.com.[^sw0204][^st0207] CTS advertising in late 2002 and early 2003 listed the block,
made with Paragon, among its new products alongside the [System 6](../timers/system-6.md) and
the [Championship Start System](../starter/championship-start-system.md).[^st0210]

The website described the design as patent pending.[^site] The patent granted was a design patent,
D471,945, for the block's appearance. It was filed on 13 June 2002, issued in March 2003, and
assigned to both companies. Its six inventors include Saldarelli.[^patent] No utility patent on
the block has been found.

CTS kept the block in its RJP guide and catalogues from 2004 to 2015.[^f507][^cat08][^cat11][^cat15]
Installations named by CTS include the University of North Carolina's Koury Natatorium, with a
System 6 and QuickConnect deck plates, and Grosse Pointe South High School, whose pool
opened in September 2008 with Colorado Blocks that CTS calls customized by Paragon.[^unc][^gp] CTS's
2015 catalogue also lists the [Fusion Starting Platform](fusion-starting-platform.md), a later
block built with Spectrum Aquatics.[^cat15] By 2013 the coloradoblock.com address served pages
from the main CTS site.[^site2013]

## Design and components

This section describes what the block is made of.

The base is a Paragon starting platform: a stainless steel frame with a top of solid
polypropylene, 1-1/4 in thick, which Paragon describes as having a patented grooved non-skid
sand finish. Its Track Start top measures 24 in across and 32 in deep, and Paragon supplies tops tilted
unless a level one is ordered.[^paragon] The RJP sits on top of that surface, and the 2002 release
notes that both have non-skid surfaces.[^pr2002]

CTS's documents describe the timing equipment in slightly different terms over time. The 2004 RJP
guide and the 2008 catalogue name a lighted RJP, an under-block speaker and the connection hub.
The 2011 and 2015 catalogues name the RJP with speedlights and the under-block start
speaker.[^f507][^cat08][^cat11][^cat15]

Paragon sells the fittings as an adapter kit. A stainless steel sponsor panel fits on slide
brackets along the side of the platform and can carry a school or sponsor logo. The kit also
carries the brackets for the horn and the deck plate (the connection hub), and the panel slides
open to reach them. It comes off the platform without tools, and comes with fixing hardware,
quick connectors, instructions and a drill bit for fitting it to an existing block. Paragon fits
it at the factory or as a field upgrade.[^paragon] The 2002 release gives the reasons for the
layout: the connection plate no longer sits on the deck where it can be damaged, and the RJP, horn and plate can
all be taken off for dry storage between meets.[^pr2002]

This section lists the platforms Paragon offers the kit for.

| Long Reach Track Start Competitor, rear mount | `22671D`[^paragon] | `46-412.LA` (rear mount)[^recrear] |

| Long Reach Competitor | `22673D`[^paragon] | Not listed |
| Standard Paraflyte or Quickset (single leg) | `22674D`[^paragon] | Not listed |

The side-mount and rear-mount versions differ in how far back the anchor sits from the pool wall.
Paragon offers the side-step Track Start Competitor with the anchor 18 or 24 in back, and the long
reach rear-mount version, meant for deck-level pools whose overflow gutters are wide, with the anchor
36–40 in back.[^paragon][^recrear] Recreonics lists shipping weights of 85 lb for the side mount and 95 lb
for the rear mount, both without anchors.[^recside][^recrear]

The dealer's sponsor panel kits differ on the horn. The rear-mount kit is listed as including
it, while the side-mount kit has only the brackets, with the horn and deck plate sold
separately.[^recpanel] Paragon's catalogue lists the kit with horn and deck plate brackets and
does not settle the point.[^paragon]

## Installation and mounting

This section covers anchoring the block and fixing the RJP.

The twin-leg Competitor platforms drop into a dual-wedge anchor set in the deck. Paragon uses a
6 in anchor where the setback from the wall is under 30 in and a 10 in anchor for 30–40 in. The
wedge is tightened by hand, so the platform goes in and out without tools.[^paragon] Recreonics lists
the block without its anchor.[^recside][^recrear]

The sources disagree on how the RJP is held on. CTS's 2004 RJP guide says RJPs for the Colorado
Block have stainless bolts welded to their underside, which go through holes drilled in the block
and are held by nuts beneath it.[^f507] Paragon and Recreonics describe the RJP as fixed in a way
that needs no tools.[^paragon][^recside] The RJP raises the standing surface by about 0.3 in.[^arch]

The Colorado Block website carried two CTS drawings from May 2002 for the deck wiring. Each lane's
deck box, a 4 × 4 × 6 in box with a drain line, sits in the shadow of its block. A 2 in conduit
links the boxes to a pull box and WLP-T wall plate at the timing table, mounted 18–36 in above the
floor, and the conduit can be carried on to the far end for splits.[^roughin][^deckplate]

## Connection to the timer

This section covers how the block's equipment reaches the timer.

The block's connection hub is CTS's [QuickConnect](../deck-cabling/cts-quickconnect-deck-plate.md)
system, which CTS credits with removing extra deck cables.[^cat11] The RJP plugs into the hub's
Button A/RJP jack with a stackable plug, and the plug of backup button A goes into the
back of it.[^f507][^f968] The speedlights flash with the start only when they are connected through the
hub to a CTS Championship Start or a later CTS start system; without that link the RJP still
works but the lights stay off.[^f507][^f968]
