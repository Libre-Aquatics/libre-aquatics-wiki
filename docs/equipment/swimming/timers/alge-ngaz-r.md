This article is a stub. The `NGAZ/R` is the [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) mains
power supply that feeds several display boards at once, such as the stacked
[GAZ4](../../common/scoreboard/alge-gaz4.md) boards of a ranking display, each over an Amphenol
cable.

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console.
