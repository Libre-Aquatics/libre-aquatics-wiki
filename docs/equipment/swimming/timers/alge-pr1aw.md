This article is a stub. The `PR1aW` is an [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md)
photocell, the PR1a with a built-in [WTN](alge-wtn.md) radio, which can send its timing impulses to
a timer by radio or over a cable.

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background on timing consoles.
