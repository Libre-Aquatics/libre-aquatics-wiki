This article is a stub. The `Q34` is the [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) headset
that connects to a [Timer S4](alge-timer-s4.md) or a [Start Acoustic SA1](../starter/alge-sa1.md)
so that the starter and the timekeeper can speak over the timing lines.

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console.
