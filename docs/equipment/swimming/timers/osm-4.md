This article is a stub. The OSM-4 is a portable Omega swim timing console for six to ten lanes,
first shown at the 1973 Canada Games, that counted laps and judged places itself but printed
results on a separate digital printer.

It was followed by the [OSM-5](osm-5.md), which put the printer in the console's case; see the
[timers overview](index.md) for the shared background.
