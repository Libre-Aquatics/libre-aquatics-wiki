This article is a stub. The OSM-5 is a portable Omega swim timing console introduced in 1975,
sold as a complete printing touchpad system for six, eight or ten lanes with
[OCP-5](../touchpad/ocp5.md) touchpads, which printed each lane's splits and finishes as they
happened and could drive a scoreboard.

It was followed by the [OSM6](osm6.md); see the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared
background.
