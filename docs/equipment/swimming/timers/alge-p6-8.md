This article is a stub. The `P6-8` is the [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) thermal
printer that keeps a running log of impulses from a [TM-SWIM](alge-tm-swim.md), which also
powers it.

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console.
