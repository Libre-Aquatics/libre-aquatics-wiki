This article is a stub. The PowerTime was an Omega Electronics hand-held timer with a printer,
in service by 1999, whose TOP-8 program and swimming harness (`3361.611`) carried over to its
successor, the [PowerTime II](powertime-ii.md).

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console.
