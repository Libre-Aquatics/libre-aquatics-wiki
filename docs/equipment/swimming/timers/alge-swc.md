This article is a stub. The SWC is an older [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md)
swimming controller, named on the [TM-SWIM](alge-tm-swim.md) product page as a device that must
not receive TM-SWIM firmware.

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console.
