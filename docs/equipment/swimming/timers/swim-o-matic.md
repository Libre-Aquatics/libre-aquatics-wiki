This article is a stub. Swim-O-Matic was Omega's name for its automatic swim timing system,
advertised in North America in 1973 as a touchpad system that printed splits and finals to the
thousandth of a second, and it remained the heading printed by the [OSM6](osm6.md) console in
the 1980s.

Its later consoles included the [OSM-5](osm-5.md); see the [timers overview](index.md) for the
shared background.
