This article is a stub. The `TRP-C08E` is a [Trycom Technology](../../../vendors/trycom.md) USB
converter with two RS232 and two RS422/485 ports, listed by Trycom from 2023 with nearly the same
specification as the later [TRP-C08H](trycom-trp-c08h.md) but no stated isolation.
