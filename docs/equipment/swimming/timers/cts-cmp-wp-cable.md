This article is a stub. The `CMP-WPxx` cable runs from the 10-pin wallplate connector of a
[Championship](../starter/championship-start-system.md) or
[Championship Elite](../starter/championship-elite-start-system.md) start system to the
start-system socket on a [CTS wall plate](../deck-cabling/cts-wall-plate.md), carrying the start signal, the
speaker audio, the speedlight signal and external strobe power on one cable.

See the [deck cabling overview](../deck-cabling/index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
deck plate and wall plate.
