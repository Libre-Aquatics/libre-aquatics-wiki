This article is a stub. The `Comet` is an [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) handheld
timer and data terminal, sold with a range of programs, that can send times and points to a
[GAZ4](../../common/scoreboard/alge-gaz4.md) display board and draw its power from it.

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console.
