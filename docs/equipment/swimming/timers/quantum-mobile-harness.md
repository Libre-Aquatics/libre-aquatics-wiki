This article is a stub. The Quantum Mobile Harness is a chain of Swiss Timing lane modules, one
per lane and up to ten per pool end, that collects the touchpad, starting-block and pushbutton
contacts on deck and carries them on one cable to a [Quantum Aquatics](quantum.md) timer.

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background.
