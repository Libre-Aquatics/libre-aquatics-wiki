This article is a stub. The `MC9` is an [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md)
multichannel unit that the TrackTimer program needs for lane timing with a
[Timy](alge-timy.md), and that ALGE's 2026 catalogue pairs with a Timy3 WP for open water
swimming.

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background on timing consoles.
