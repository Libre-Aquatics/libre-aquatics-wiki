This article is a stub. WTN, the Wireless Timing Network, is the
[ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) 2.4 GHz radio system built into the
[Timy3](alge-timy.md), which links it to radio push buttons (`WTN-PB`), start devices and display
boards over about 350 m in line of sight.

In swimming it carries the finish buttons of ALGE's radio semi-automatic system and the judges'
scores at diving events; see the [Timy](alge-timy.md) article for both uses.
