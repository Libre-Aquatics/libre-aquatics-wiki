Surfaced 2026-11-04 while building out alge-tm-swim.md.

HS4-1 product page: single-sided, 1/8 W, 200 ohm microphone, 400 ohm earpiece, 1.5 m cable, usable
with the SU3, TM-SWIM, SV4 speech amplifiers and STSn start gates. SU3 page lists HS4-2 as the
double-sided model; no separate HS4-2 page read. The SU3 manual gives the 5-pin DIN headset pinout
(microphone pin 1, common pin 2, earphone pin 3) and says a connected headset turns off the
internal microphone. Older: headset Q34 on the Timer S4. BANG-HS is a different, radio headset.

This article is a stub. The `HS4-1` and `HS4-2` are
[ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) single-sided and double-sided headsets that
connect to the [SU3](../starter/alge-su3.md) and the [TM-SWIM](alge-tm-swim.md).

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console.
