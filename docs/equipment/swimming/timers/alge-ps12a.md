This article is a stub. The `PS12A` is an [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) mains
power supply with a DC plug that runs and charges [Timy](alge-timy.md) timers and printers and is
the only charger for the [E-START W](../starter/alge-e-start.md).

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console.
