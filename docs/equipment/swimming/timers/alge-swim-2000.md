This article is a stub. The SWIM 2000 is an [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) swim
timer that connects to [Hy-Tek Meet Manager](../../../software/hy-tek-meet-manager.md) by a
serial cable from its Printer 2 port.

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console, and the [TM-SWIM](alge-tm-swim.md) for the current ALGE console.
