What is known, all second-hand:

    the S3 had been in use around the world for more than ten years. It tells S3 owners that the
    S4's program 0 behaves like the S3's split program, and that program 3 with time of day works

  - FIS homologated timing equipment list "valid as of 22/10/2010" (PDF dated 22 October 2010,
    copy hosted by Reliable Racing): ALG.004.97, S3, with external printer, valid until 2011. The

  - TimingGuys forum thread "ALGE timing software vs S4" (23 March 2007): a reply gives the S3's
    serial format as 2400 baud, no parity, 7 data bits, 2 stop bits, against 4800 baud 8N1 on the

    Forum post, unverified.

Not established: introduction date (the ten-year remark puts it at least ten years before the S4,

This article is a stub. The Timer S3 is the [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md)
multi-sport timer that the [Timer S4](alge-timer-s4.md) replaced, and FIS listed it as
homologated timing equipment under `ALG.004.97` until 2011.

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console.
