This article is a stub. The `GTT15` and `GTT25` are [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md)
carrying bags for six-digit [GAZ5](../../common/scoreboard/alge-gaz5.md) and
[D-LINE](../../common/scoreboard/alge-d-line.md) display boards with 15 cm and 25 cm digits.

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console.
