This article is a stub. The Kyroscope was a portable, battery-powered automatic timing and
place-judging console for swimming and track, sold by [Taroda Industries](../../../vendors/taroda-industries.md)
from 1969 in 4, 6 and 8-lane models and later as the Kyroscope Mark II printing timer.

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing console.
