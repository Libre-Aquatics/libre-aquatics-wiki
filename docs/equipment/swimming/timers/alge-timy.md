This article is a stub. The Timy is the [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) hand-held
timer series, and the nine-channel Timy3 WP (`TIMY3 WP-S`) is the timing device in ALGE
semi-automatic swimming systems.

See the [semi-automatic timing overview](../semi-automatic/index.md) for that class of
equipment, and the [TM-SWIM](alge-tm-swim.md) for the fully automatic ALGE system.
