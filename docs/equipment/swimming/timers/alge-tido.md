This article is a stub. The `TIDO` is an [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) docking
station for the [Timy3](alge-timy.md) that adds a speech amplifier and brings the timer's nine
timing channels out to banana sockets, alongside photocell, RS232 and headset sockets.

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background on timing consoles.
