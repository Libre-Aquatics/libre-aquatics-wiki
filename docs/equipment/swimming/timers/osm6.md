This article is a stub. The OSM6 is an Omega swim timing console, used with
[OCP5](../touchpad/ocp5.md) touchpads at the 1984 Olympic Games, that could send results to
[Hy-Tek Meet Manager](../../../software/hy-tek-meet-manager.md) through an optional data
handling module; its serial results format survived as the OSM6 protocol in Swiss Timing's
[ARES 21](ares-21.md) and [Quantum](quantum.md) systems.

It followed the [OSM-5](osm-5.md); see the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared
background.
