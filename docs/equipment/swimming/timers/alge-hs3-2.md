This article is a stub. The `HS3-2` is the [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) headset
that ALGE's 2015 start-devices brochure and the [SU3](../starter/alge-su3.md) manual show for
talking to the timing operator and giving commands through an SU3 or BANG start system.

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console.
