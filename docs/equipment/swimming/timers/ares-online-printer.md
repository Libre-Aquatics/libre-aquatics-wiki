This article is a stub. The ARES online printer, part number `3330.630` with a built-in battery
or `3330.661` for mains power, is the serial printer that plugs into the
[ARES 21](ares-21.md) interface and prints each time as it is recorded, and can also print
results at the end of a race.

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console.
