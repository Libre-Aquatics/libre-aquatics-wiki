This article is a stub. The Tec-Timer was a printing meet timer for swimming, diving and track made
by [Kyrotech](../../../vendors/kyrotech.md), a 10-lane, menu-programmed unit with a built-in
thermal printer that Hy-Tek's Meet Manager still lists among supported consoles.

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console.
