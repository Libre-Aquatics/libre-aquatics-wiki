This article is a stub. The `TRP-C08D` is a [Trycom Technology](../../../vendors/trycom.md) USB
converter with one RS232 port and one RS422/485 port, a two-port member of the TRP-C08 series that
also includes the [TRP-C08H](trycom-trp-c08h.md).

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console.
