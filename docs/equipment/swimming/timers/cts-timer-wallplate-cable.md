This article is a stub. The `CHI-xx-CPUA` cable joins the timing sockets for each pool end on a
[CTS wall plate](../deck-cabling/cts-wall-plate.md) to the rear panel of a parallel-wired CTS timing console such
as the [Gen7 Legacy Timer](gen7-legacy.md), with a `CHI12-xx` cable added for lanes 11
and 12; CTS sells it in a 25 ft length as `CHI-25-CPUA`.

See the [deck cabling overview](../deck-cabling/index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
deck plate and wall plate.
