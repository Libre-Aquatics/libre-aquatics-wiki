This article is a stub. The PowerTime II is a hand-held Swiss Timing timer with a built-in printer,
article `3361.900`, which can take pushbuttons through a nine-box swimming harness and, loaded with
Swiss Timing's TOP-8 software, run a small swimming meet and drive a
[Piccolo](../../common/scoreboard/piccolo.md) scoreboard.

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console.
