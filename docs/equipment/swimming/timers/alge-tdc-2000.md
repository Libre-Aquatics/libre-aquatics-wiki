This article is a stub. The `TdC 2000` is a late-1970s
[ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) timing computer that preceded the
[TdC 4000](alge-tdc-4000.md); the company claims it was the first timer to process data.

See the [timers overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console.
