The smaller items that serve scoreboards, scoreboard controllers and pace clocks, or that
are used in more than one discipline.

## What lives here

- Hand switches that run a game clock, a shot clock or a horn away from the console.
- Junction boxes and cables for scoreboard and pace clock wiring.
- Power supplies for display boards.
- Serial converters and computer links.
- Bags and wheeled frames for display boards.

Adapters and receivers that carry data to a board are under
[scoreboard control](../scoreboard-control/index.md).
