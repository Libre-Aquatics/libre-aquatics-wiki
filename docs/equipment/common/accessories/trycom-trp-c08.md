This article is a stub. The `TRP-C08` is a [Trycom Technology](../../../vendors/trycom.md) USB
converter with a single port that works as RS232, RS422 or RS485, the one-port model of the series
that also includes the four-port [TRP-C08H](trycom-trp-c08h.md).

See the [timers overview](../../swimming/timers/index.md) for the shared background that applies to every timing
console.
