This article is a stub. The Daktronics `WP-2101` is a 9 ft (2.74 m) wide water polo scoreboard
showing game time, scores, period, shot time and timeouts left, controlled from an
[All Sport 5000](../scoreboard-control/all-sport-5000.md) and extended by the
[WP-2102](wp-2102.md) penalty module.

See the [scoreboards overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
scoreboard.
