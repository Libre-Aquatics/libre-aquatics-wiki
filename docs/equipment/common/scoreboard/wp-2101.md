The WP-2101 is a water polo scoreboard made by [Daktronics](../../../vendors/daktronics.md). It
shows the game clock, each team's score, the period, the shot clock and each team's timeouts
left.[^manual][^product] It is controlled from an
[All Sport 5000](../scoreboard-control/all-sport-5000.md) console or an
[OmniSport 2000](../../swimming/timers/omnisport-2000.md) timing console.[^manual] The
[WP-2102](wp-2102.md) is a separate penalty module that mounts below it.[^manual] Daktronics
lists the LED board covered by its 2007 manual as made from 1999 to 2014, and it still sells a
board under the same model number.[^discontinued][^product]

## Role in a water polo setup

This section places the WP-2101 among the other pieces of a Daktronics water polo setup. The operator runs the
game clock, shot clock, scores, period and timeouts from the console, and the board's driver
lights the digits.[^manual][^as5000]

On the All Sport 5000, water polo is sport code 4498 with insert `0G-178439`.[^as5000] On the
OmniSport 2000 it is mode 4, with its own insert, and the console has separate ports for game
clock and shot clock switches.[^os2000] Both consoles also keep penalty timers for excluded
players. The WP-2101 has no penalty fields, so those times appear only when a WP-2102 is
fitted.[^manual][^as5000][^os2000] The manual allows the WP-2101 to run alone, with a WP-2102,
or beside other Daktronics swimming scoreboards. Daktronics' support page also lists a portable
battery-powered game and shot clock, the [WP-2103](wp-2103.md), in the same family.[^manual][^support]

## Design and hardware

The cabinet is aluminum, 3 ft 6 in (1.07 m) high and 9 ft (2.74 m) wide. The face carries
HOME and GUEST captions over the scores, with the game clock between them. The period digit
sits in the middle row, and the lower row holds both timeouts-left digits and the two-digit shot
clock.[^drawing][^product] The board weighs 100 lb (46 kg) as mounted and 160 lb (73 kg) as
shipped.[^drawing] Daktronics' specification gives its power as 200 W.[^spec]

Its 15 digits are fixed seven-segment digits, each wired to one of the 16 outputs of a single
LED driver.[^manual][^driver][^drawing] The game clock and scores use 10 in digits. The period, timeouts
left and shot clock use 7 in digits in the 2002 drawings and the 2007 manual. Daktronics'
2018 aquatics brochure, current product page and architect specification all give 8 in digits
for those fields instead, so the smaller size belongs to the older LED
series.[^drawing][^brochure][^product][^spec]

The current page lists these display ranges:[^product]

| Field | Range |
|---|---|
| Game time | Up to 99:59, with tenths of a second under one minute |
| Home and guest score | Up to 199 |
| Period | Up to 9 |
| Shot time | Up to 99 |
| Timeouts left | One digit per team |

## Models and digit colors

This section covers the model suffixes and LED colors. In the 2007 manual the suffix names the
setting and the color:[^manual]

| Model | Setting | Digits |
|---|---|---|
| `WP-2101-13` | Indoor | Red or amber |
| `WP-2101-11` | Outdoor | Red-orange |
| `WP-2101-21` | Outdoor | Amber |

The manual gives versions for 120 V and 230 V supplies.[^manual] Daktronics' current page sells
one outdoor design in red or amber, with an option that changes the digit color to show which
team leads or what the clock is doing. For indoor use it lists an optional power and signal
kit.[^product]

## Control and wiring

This section covers how the board receives power and signal. The console's signal reaches the
board on a pair of wires. Daktronics calls for at least 22 AWG shielded two-conductor cable, and
its parts list includes molded signal cables from 10 to 100 ft.[^manual][^drawing] Indoor boards
in the 2007 series have a power cord and a signal jack, `J31`, on the left end. Outdoor boards
take power through conduit and signal cable through knockouts in the rear.[^manual] The
architect specification describes a wired link to a control junction box, with a 2.4 GHz radio
as an option, and the 2018 brochure also offers radio control to cut cabling.[^spec][^brochure]
The brochure says the board works with other manufacturers' timing equipment, without naming
any.[^brochure]

The driver sits in an enclosure with its transformer and signal terminal block, behind the home
score digits.[^manual] It has 16 outputs for digits and connectors for power and signal, the horn
and an address setting.[^driver] The WP-2101's driver is set to address 13, and the WP-2102's
driver to address 14, so the console can address each board separately.[^drawing][^drawing2102] The driver
drawing notes that a driver with no address pins set falls back to the protocol of the older All
Sport 4000.[^driver] On outdoor boards a horn plate sits at the top center of the
cabinet.[^drawing]

## Clock settings

This section summarizes the console settings that shape what the board shows. On the All Sport
5000 the factory defaults are 15-minute periods, a 35-second shot reset and a 20-second penalty
time, with the shot clock tied to the game clock and blanked when it would exceed the time left
in the period.[^as5000] Those defaults do not match the current rules. World Aquatics now plays four
periods of eight minutes of actual play and allows 28 seconds of possession, so an operator sets
the period and shot reset times in the console's settings menu before a game.[^wacr][^as5000] The
shot clock can sound with the main horn or on its own, and on both consoles a recall key restores the
shot clock value that an accidental reset wiped out.[^as5000][^os2000]

## Installation

This section summarizes the mounting options in the manual. The board can be bolted flat to a
wall on mounting struts with 3/8 in bolts, set across an indoor corner on optional brackets, or
mounted outdoors on two steel beams 4 ft 6 in apart, center to center.[^manual] A
qualified engineer has to specify the beams and their footings. The beam clamps use threaded
rods that run along the sides of each beam, so the beams are not drilled.[^manual] Where a
WP-2102 is fitted, it is hung first and the WP-2101 is set on top, with the two cabinets bolted
together and the WP-2102's power and signal passed up through holes in the joining
panels.[^manual][^drawing] The lifting eye on top is for installation only and must not carry the
board permanently.[^drawing]

## Care and maintenance

The board is serviced from the front through hinged digit panels.[^manual] The manual says not to
replace single LEDs. A digit with a failed LED or segment is changed as a whole panel, using a
keyed nine-pin connector.[^manual] The manual's troubleshooting table traces a blank board to
power or the console connection, a garbled board to the driver or console, and dead or stuck
segments to the digit, its wiring or the driver.[^manual] For lightning protection, Daktronics
recommends a disconnect near the board and switching off the power when the board is not in
use. The console should likewise be unplugged, at both its power supply and its signal
connection, whenever it is idle.[^manual]

## Part numbers and accessories

This section lists the parts named in the 2007 manual.[^manual]

- `0P-1150-0127`, the 16-column LED driver (`0P-1150-0126` uncoated).[^driver]
- `T-1066`, the 16 V transformer.
- `0A-1152-0332`, the 120 V AC horn.
- `0A-1009-0038`, a phone-jack junction box for the console connection, and `W-1077`, one-pair 22 AWG cable.
- `W-1340`, `W-1236`, `W-1238`, `W-1237` and `W-1381`, signal cables of 10, 20, 30, 50 and 100 ft.
