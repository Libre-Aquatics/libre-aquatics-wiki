This article is a stub. The Daktronics `WP-2102` is a 9 ft (2.74 m) wide add-on module that
shows each team's player penalties beside a [WP-2101](wp-2101.md) water polo scoreboard,
controlled from an [All Sport 5000](../scoreboard-control/all-sport-5000.md).

See the [scoreboards overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
scoreboard.
