This article is a stub. The UNT4 is an Omega multi-line scoreboard named among the equipment of
the 1984 Olympic Games and driven by the [OSM6](../../swimming/timers/osm6.md) console, and its
name lives on as the UNT4 protocol that Swiss Timing systems use to drive boards such as the
[Calypso](calypso.md).

See the [scoreboards overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
scoreboard.
