This article is a stub. The Piccolo is an indoor Swiss Timing LED scoreboard of eight lines of
twelve 10 cm characters, which uses the [Calypso](calypso.md) protocol to show running times,
reaction times, splits and ranked results from a [Quantum Aquatics](../../swimming/timers/quantum.md)
timer, and can serve as a repeater board away from the pool.

See the [scoreboards overview](index.md) for the shared background.
