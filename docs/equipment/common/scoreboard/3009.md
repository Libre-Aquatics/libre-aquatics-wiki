This article is a stub. The `3009` is a Colorado Time Systems numeric scoreboard series that
CTS documents of 1995 and 2004 list beside the [4009](4009.md) as a board its timers and the
[Ultimate Pace Clock](../pace-clock/ultimate-pace-clock.md) console could drive.

See the [scoreboards overview](index.md) for the shared background.
