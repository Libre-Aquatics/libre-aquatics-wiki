Swimming World, June 1985 (page image checked, p. 67), also July and August 1985: Kyrotech
"Tec-Timer Databoard" ad. A public display showing, on one line, the running time, lane, lap
count, splits, finishing place, final times, and the winning lane and time shown automatically;

team scores and for meet records. The illustration shows a "LANE / LAP-LGTH COMPLETED / PLACE" section

The September 1992 Kyrotech ad lists "1 to 8 lane Time Scoreboards" and alphanumeric team

This article is a stub. The Databoard was a single-line display made by
[Kyrotech](../../../vendors/kyrotech.md) for its [Tec-Timer](../../swimming/timers/kyrotech-tec-timer.md),
showing lane, lap, place and time to 0.01 s with either 9 in mechanical or 13 in incandescent
digits.

See the [scoreboard overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
scoreboard.
