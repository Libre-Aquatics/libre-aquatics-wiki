This article is a stub. The `4009` is a Colorado Time Systems light-reflective numeric
scoreboard built from 102 in modules, each with eight 9 in seven-segment digits whose positions
slide to suit the sport, driven by CTS swim timers and by the
[Ultimate Pace Clock](../pace-clock/ultimate-pace-clock.md) console.

See the [scoreboards overview](index.md) for the shared background, and the
[LED-R](led-r.md) article for the LED boards documented alongside it.
