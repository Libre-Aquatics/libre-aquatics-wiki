This article is a stub. D-SW is the [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) family of
numeric LED swimming scoreboards, driven by a [TM-SWIM](../../swimming/timers/alge-tm-swim.md)
and built as single-line or multi-line boards showing rank, lane and time.

See the [scoreboards overview](index.md) for the shared background on scoreboards.
