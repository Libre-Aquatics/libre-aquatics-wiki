Research notes, LED-REX.

Single attestation, and a short one. The 2011 CTS catalogue
(start-systems/cts-complete-timing-scoring-training-and-display-solutions-2011-2011.txt,
line 665) heads a block LED-REX and offers it as a way to bring an installed board of the
older, light-reflective kind up to LED digits, directing the reader to a sales consultant
for details. Nothing else is said: no part numbers, no price, no statement of
which boards qualify or what the work involves.

The name is not in any manual held. F218, which documents both the LED boards and the 4009
light-reflective board, never mentions a retrofit, and F1079, the 2026 guide that replaces
it, has dropped the light-reflective material altogether.

What makes this worth a page rather than a footnote: it is the only evidence held that CTS
offered an upgrade path between the two digit technologies, which matters for dating the
changeover. Read it as a 2011 offer, not as something currently sold.

Still to research: what the retrofit consisted of, whether the frame and signage were kept,
which boards it fitted, what it cost, when it was offered from and until, and whether any
facility record of one survives. A CTS price list or a dealer catalogue from the period is
the likeliest source.

This article is a stub. LED-REX is a Colorado Time Systems retrofit that converted an
installed light-reflective scoreboard to LED digits, offered in the company's 2011 catalogue
with no detail beyond an invitation to ask a sales consultant.

See the [LED-R](led-r.md) article for the LED modules it upgraded boards to, and the
[scoreboards overview](index.md) for the difference between the two digit technologies.
