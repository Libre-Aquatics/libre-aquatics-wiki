This article is a stub. Calypso is a Swiss Timing numeric scoreboard built from eight-digit lines
of 24 cm white LED digits, a master line driving up to three slave lines, which takes its data
over RS422 from a [Quantum Aquatics](../../swimming/timers/quantum.md) timer, an ARES system or a
water polo controller.

See the [scoreboards overview](index.md) for the shared background.
