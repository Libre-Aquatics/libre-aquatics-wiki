This article is a stub. The mini scoreboard caddies, `CAD-MINI-4` and `CAD-MINI-5`, are
wheeled frames that hold up to four or five Colorado Time Systems 5 inch digit scoreboard
modules, or a multi-line swimming or diving board of equivalent size, so that the display
can be rolled out for a session and away afterwards.

See the [mini LED scoreboard](mini-scoreboard.md) article for the boards they carry, and
the [scoreboards overview](index.md) for the shared background.
