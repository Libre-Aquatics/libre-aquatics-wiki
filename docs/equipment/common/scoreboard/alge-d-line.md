This article is a stub. D-LINE is the [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md) series of
numeric LED display boards, made in three- and six-digit standard models, that ALGE's 2026
swimming catalogue uses to show the time from a [Timy](../../swimming/timers/alge-timy.md) in its
semi-automatic systems.

See the [scoreboards overview](index.md) for the shared background on scoreboards.
