This article is a stub. The TI-2101 is a Daktronics indoor timing display with four 13 in LED
digits in a 2 × 4 ft cabinet, offered as a segment timer, time-of-day clock, pace clock and
water polo shot clock and controlled from All Sport consoles.

Daktronics suggests it for buyers who want larger digits than the portable
[PC-2001](pc-2001.md) has; see the [pace clocks overview](index.md) for the shared background.
