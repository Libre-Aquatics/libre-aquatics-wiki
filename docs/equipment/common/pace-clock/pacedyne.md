This article is a stub. The Pacedyne was a [Sycom](../../../vendors/sycom.md) pace clock with a
quartz movement that ran on one C cell, sold by [Seagull
Enterprises](../../../vendors/seagull.md) from 1977.

See the [pace clocks overview](index.md) for the shared background on pace clocks.
