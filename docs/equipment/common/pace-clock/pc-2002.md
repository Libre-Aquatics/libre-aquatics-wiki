The PC-2002 is an LED pace clock made by [Daktronics](../../../vendors/daktronics.md) for
permanent mounting on an indoor wall. It was designed in 2003 as the fixed counterpart of the
portable [PC-2001](pc-2001.md), and it takes mains power instead of a battery.[^manual] It
has seven programs: a workout pace clock, a count-up timer, a game clock, a shot clock, two
time-of-day formats and a receive-only mode. It is set up from the
[JC-100](../../diving/jc-100.md) handheld console, from an All Sport or
[OmniSport 2000](../../swimming/timers/omnisport-2000.md) console by cable or radio, or by
downloading workouts from Hy-Tek software.[^manual] See the [pace clocks overview](index.md)
for the background shared by every pace clock.

## Models and history

The PC-2002 belongs to the same Daktronics product family (P1153) as the PC-2001 and the
[TI-2022](ti-2022.md) timer, and its drawings date from 2003. Its schematic was drawn on
29 April 2003, the riser diagram for a permanent installation on 7 July 2003, and the diagram
showing both clocks receiving Hy-Tek workouts on 19 August 2003. Every edition of the manual
carries a copyright from 2003.[^manual]

The manual's editions record changes to the product:

- Revision 2, of September 2012, describes the clock as running on 120 V only. It offers
  surface and flush mounting and has no radio section.[^rev2]
- Revision 5, of December 2019, drops flush mounting and adds the radio receiver and its
  adapter cable.[^rev5]
- Revision 6, of August 2021, is the current edition. It covers 120 V and 240 V models.[^manual]

The 240 V schematic is dated December 2010, so a 240 V clock existed before the 2012 manual
that describes only 120 V.[^manual][^rev2]

Daktronics' 2018 aquatics brochure offers the PC-2002 as the indoor clock for a permanent
mounting, alongside the portable PC-2001.[^brochure] As of 2026 the manual is still published.
Daktronics has said it is ending the OmniSport timing system, one of the consoles that can
drive the clock, with no new orders and up to seven more years of service.[^kbshot]

## Design and hardware

The clock's face holds four seven-segment LED digits. Daktronics' riser drawing shows a cabinet
24 in wide and 9.7 in tall, with an 8.5 in face.[^manual] No edition of the manual gives the
digit height, the depth or the weight. The display's brightness can be set in sixteen steps,
and the brightest is the default.[^manual]

The cabinet is made of front and rear panels held together by ten screws. A single circuit
board (`0P-1153-0005`) is bolted to the back of the front panel. It carries the LEDs, the
processors, the power input and the horn, and every other connection runs to it. A transformer
(`T-1082` on 120 V models) is also mounted inside.[^manual]

Unlike the PC-2001, the PC-2002 has no switches, jacks or battery-status lamps on its sides in
the 2021 manual's figures and drawings. All connections are made at the back:[^manual]

- A ten-position terminal block. Two positions take the signal input and two the signal
  output to further clocks. Four carry the CAN bus (high, low, power and ground) that links the
  clock to a JC-100.
- A fixed power cord (`0A-1153-0038`) with a three-prong plug.

Its safety approvals cover indoor use only: an ETL listing, testing to CSA standards and a CE
label.[^manual] The manual's safety instructions also say that it
must not be dripped or splashed on.[^manual]

## Installation and mounting

Daktronics leaves the mounting to the customer. The anchors are not supplied, and the manual
asks for a qualified engineer to choose them to suit local building codes.[^manual] The clock
is surface-mounted. Brackets fixed to its top and bottom edges are held by four wall anchors of
3/16–1/4 in; for steel anchors the minimum is 3/16 in (4.7 mm). A mains receptacle is
fitted close to the clock, where it meets the openings in the back of the cabinet.[^manual]

The 2012 manual also described flush mounting. The clock sat in a 25.5 × 10.0 in opening cut
into the wall, with a bezel fixed to the wall by six anchors and to the clock by eight screws.
The flush-mount hardware had its own part number (`0A-1153-0411`).[^rev2] Flush mounting is
absent from the 2019 and 2021 editions.[^rev5][^manual]

## Power

The clock plugs into a 120 V or 240 V AC outlet; the label on the cabinet gives the rating for
each unit.[^manual] An internal time-delay fuse protects it, and Daktronics asks owners to
contact customer service rather than replace the fuse themselves.[^manual]

The fuse ratings disagree within the 2021 manual. The text gives 1/2 A at 250 V for 120 V models
and 1/4 A for 240 V models. The schematics, revised in January 2016 to add the fuse, show 1/4 A
(`F-1079`) on the 120 V clock and 1/8 A (`F-1080`) on the 240 V clock.[^manual] The 2019 edition
gives the same figures in its text as the 2021 one.[^rev5]

## Programs

The PC-2002 has seven programs. The swimming reaction and relay exchange timers of the PC-2001
are not among them, and the programs are numbered differently: the workout program is number 1
here and number 5 on the PC-2001.[^manual][^pc2001]

| No. | Program | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Workout ("pace clock") | Counts up to a pace time between 00:00 and 99:59, sounds the horn and repeats; with no time set it runs to 59:59 and starts again. Also runs programmed workouts. |
| 2 | Count-up or breakout timer | Counts up to a set time and sounds the horn. A start signal while running shows a split for ten seconds; on the JC-100 each split is numbered as a lap, up to 99. |
| 3 | Game clock | Counts down from a set time (up to 99:59) to zero and sounds the horn |
| 4 | Shot clock | Counts down from 0–99 seconds on the middle two digits and sounds the horn |
| 5 | Time of day, 12-hour | Clock display |
| 6 | Time of day, 24-hour | Clock display |
| 7 | Slave | Shows only what an All Sport or OmniSport console sends; blank with no signal |

Program numbers and behavior follow the 2021 manual. The 2012 edition lists the same seven.[^manual][^rev2]
Choosing the shot clock program also sets the clock to accept a shot clock feed from an All
Sport or OmniSport console, so it can serve as a water polo shot clock. In that program, RESET
while the clock is running restarts the countdown at once; after STOP, RESET restores the preset
and START must be pressed again.[^manual]

The JC-100's mode menu lists only programs 1 to 6. The slave program appears in the manual's
list of programs, but no edition says how it is selected.[^manual][^rev2]

### Workouts

Workouts are built on the JC-100 using the pace clock keypad insert (`LL-2584`). The steps are
the same as on the PC-2001: swim and rest steps with their own times, a pace step that repeats
one interval without end, begin and end circuit steps that bracket a repeated group, and a stop
step.[^manual] The horn can be set separately for swim and rest steps, and outside the workout
program it can be automatic or manual.[^manual] The [PC-2001 workout section](pc-2001.md#workouts)
describes the steps in more detail.

### Hy-Tek download

Hy-Tek [Team Manager](../../../software/hy-tek-team-manager.md) with the
[Workout Manager](../../../software/hy-tek-workout-manager.md) option can send a workout to the
clock. The procedure is the PC-2001's, but the cabling differs because the PC-2002's signal input is a
screw terminal rather than a phone jack. The [Hy-Tek data cable](hy-tek-data-cable.md) (`0A-1153-0036`) runs from the computer's
serial port to a [phone-jack junction box](../scoreboard-control/daktronics-phone-jack-junction-box.md)
(`0A-1091-0227`). From the box, a shielded cable goes to the signal input on the clock's
terminal block, red to terminal 1 and black to terminal 2.[^manual] The clock must be in program 1 with a
JC-100 connected. Team Manager sends only the lane 1 workout, and the clock shows `donE` when
the transfer succeeds.[^manual]

## Console and radio control

This section covers the three ways of driving the clock: the JC-100, a scoreboard or timing
console by cable, and a console by radio.

The [JC-100](../../diving/jc-100.md) does not plug into the clock directly. Its lead goes to a
[JC-100 junction box](../../diving/jc-100-junction-box.md) (`0A-1240-0027`), and from there a
two-pair 22 AWG cable (`W-1234`, drawn at 20 ft) runs to the CAN terminals on the clock. A
termination plug (`0A-1241-4010`) goes on the console's lead.[^manual] Daktronics' JC-100
specification sheet lists the PC-2002 among the displays the console operates.[^jc100spec]

Clocks are daisy-chained from the signal output of one to the signal input of the next, with up
to 1,000 ft of cable between them. Daktronics' riser drawing for a permanent installation adds
these rules:[^manual]

1. Each clock can have only one JC-100.
2. An OmniSport 2000 or All Sport console plugged into the phone-jack box at the head of the
   chain drives every clock. A JC-100 at the first clock can drive the whole chain instead.
3. There is no stated limit on the number of clocks in a chain.
4. A clock further down the chain can be run on its own only after its signal input wires are
   disconnected.

The manuals and drawings disagree on the signal cable. The text of the 2012 and 2021 editions
specifies 18 AWG shielded cable (`W-1117`). Both riser drawings were changed in October 2014 to a
different cable, `W-1077`, which the PC-2001 manual describes as single-pair shielded
22 AWG.[^manual][^rev2][^pc2001]

For radio control, an optional [Gen VI](../scoreboard-control/gen-vi-radio.md) receiver is
fitted inside the right-hand end of the cabinet. An adapter cable (`W-2913`) connects it to the
board's radio jack, and the antenna passes through a hole in the side and must point straight
up. The controlling All Sport or OmniSport 2000 console needs a radio transmitter. Once the
receiver is fitted, the signal input and output terminals can no longer be used. The clock shows
the receiver's broadcast group and channel during its power-on self test, and both must match
the console's settings.[^manual]

With an OmniSport 2000, the console's scoreboard output protocol must be set to multi-drop. If
it is left on Omega, Daktronics' knowledge base says, the count-up display goes wrong: the
right-hand digit steps once every ten seconds instead of counting seconds.[^kbomega]

## Care and maintenance

Daktronics asks that the clock be unplugged before any work on it, and that only qualified
service staff open it.[^manual] To replace the circuit board, the ten panel screws are removed,
the keyed connectors are unplugged, and the board is lifted off its eight studs and spacers with
even pressure to avoid bending the LEDs.[^manual] The company's six-monthly maintenance
checklist asks for a visual check of each pace clock's connections, a firmware check and an
operating test.[^maint]
