This article is a stub. The PC-2002 is a Daktronics LED pace clock for permanent indoor wall
mounting, powered from the mains instead of a battery and offering seven programs: workout,
count-up, game clock, shot clock, two time-of-day formats and a receive-only mode for a
console feed.

It is the fixed counterpart of the portable [PC-2001](pc-2001.md); see the
[pace clocks overview](index.md) for the shared background.
