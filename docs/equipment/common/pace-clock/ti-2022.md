This article is a stub. The TI-2022 is a Daktronics portable LED timer, in outdoor
(`TI-2022-11`) and indoor (`TI-2022-13`) models, that shares the battery cabinet and circuit
design of the [PC-2001](pc-2001.md) pace clock but replaces its swimming programs with nine
general timing programs, including photocell and pushbutton count-up timers, a lap counter and
time of day.

See the [pace clocks overview](index.md) for the background on the clocks it was built beside.
