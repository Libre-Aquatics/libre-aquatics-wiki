This article is a stub. The Daktronics Hy-Tek data cable (`0A-1153-0036`) is a 20 ft lead with a
9-pin serial plug at one end and a 1/4 in stereo phone plug at the other, used to download
workouts from Hy-Tek Team Manager into the [PC-2001](pc-2001.md) and [PC-2002](pc-2002.md)
pace clocks.

See the [pace clocks overview](index.md) for the shared background.
