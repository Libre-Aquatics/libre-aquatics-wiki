This article is a stub. The QuickStart Pro is a Colorado Time Systems portable LED training
display, the successor to the [QuickStart](quickstart.md), with fifteen modes covering pace
clock, lap counting, split and single-lane timing, and start, relay exchange, break-out and
turn timing from touchpads, a relay judging platform, a start system or a pushbutton.

See the [pace clocks overview](index.md) for the shared background.
