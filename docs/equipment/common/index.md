The hardware that is not specific to one sport but supports competitions across
swimming, diving, water polo, artistic swimming, and open water. Where the
per-discipline sections catalog the gear unique to a sport, this section covers the
shared infrastructure: the displays that show results, the console platforms that
serve more than one sport, and the cabling, audio, and data equipment that ties a
timing system together.

## What lives here

Common equipment is grouped by role:

- Scoreboards and video displays, the fixed and modular boards that show times,
  scores, and standings for any sport.
- Timing consoles, the console platforms that run more than one sport from the same
  hardware.
- Cabling and connectors, the deck plates, harnesses, and wiring that link lane and
  field hardware back to the console.
- Public address and audio, the announcer, horn, and loudspeaker equipment used at
  the pool.
- Network and data hardware, the interfaces that move results between the console,
  scoreboard, and meet-management software.
- Power and support gear, the supplies, batteries, and enclosures that keep the
  system running.
