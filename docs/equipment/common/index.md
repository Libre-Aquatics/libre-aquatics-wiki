The hardware that is not specific to one sport but supports competitions across
swimming, diving, water polo, artistic swimming, and open water. Where the
per-discipline sections catalog the gear unique to a sport, this section covers the
shared infrastructure: the displays that show results, the console platforms that
serve more than one sport, and the cabling, audio, and data equipment that ties a
timing system together.

## What lives here

Common equipment is grouped by role:

- Scoreboards and video displays, the fixed and modular boards that show times,
  scores, and standings for any sport.
- Scoreboard control, the adapters, controllers and consoles that feed a board.
- Pace clocks, the training-side displays and the controllers that program them.
- Photo finish, the camera systems that record a finish line.
- Cables and accessories, the switches, junction boxes, cables, power supplies and converters
  that go with the rest.

Deck plates, harnesses and wiring are under
[deck cabling](../swimming/deck-cabling/index.md), and consoles that time more than one sport
are under [timers](../swimming/timers/index.md).

## Articles

### Scoreboards

[Scoreboards](scoreboard/index.md) are the boards that show times, scores and event
information, covering the numeric, matrix and LED video generations.

- Colorado Time Systems: [Otter](scoreboard/otter.md), the
  [mini LED scoreboard](scoreboard/mini-scoreboard.md) and the [LED-R](scoreboard/led-r.md)
  module, the current numeric boards, with the [LED-REX](scoreboard/led-rex.md) retrofit; and
  [Myriad](scoreboard/myriad.md) and [Mercury16](scoreboard/mercury16.md), the matrix
  displays of the 1990s and 2000s.
- ALGE-Timing: the [GAZ4](scoreboard/alge-gaz4.md), [GAZ5](scoreboard/alge-gaz5.md),
  [D-LINE](scoreboard/alge-d-line.md), [D-SW](scoreboard/alge-d-sw.md) and
  [D-RTNM](scoreboard/alge-d-rtnm.md) display boards.
- Swiss Timing: [Calypso](scoreboard/calypso.md) and [Piccolo](scoreboard/piccolo.md).

Boards made only for water polo are under
[water polo scoreboards](../water-polo/scoreboard/index.md).

### Scoreboard control

[Scoreboard control](scoreboard-control/index.md) covers what feeds a board.

- [Sky-Fi WA-1](scoreboard-control/wa-1.md), [WA-2](scoreboard-control/wa-2.md),
  [WA-3](scoreboard-control/wa-3.md) and the [Gen VI Radio](scoreboard-control/gen-vi-radio.md):
  the wireless links that carry scoreboard data to a board without a cable.
- [WTTC-1](scoreboard-control/wttc-1.md), [WHC-1](scoreboard-control/whc-1.md) and the
  [All Sport 5000](scoreboard-control/all-sport-5000.md): controllers that drive a board on
  their own.
- [GameLink console](scoreboard-control/gamelink-console.md): the keyboard console that fed a
  display with team-sport data.
- [MCTRL-300](scoreboard-control/mctrl-300.md), [MCTRL-600](scoreboard-control/mctrl-600.md),
  [MCTRL-660](scoreboard-control/mctrl-660.md), [VX4](scoreboard-control/vx4.md) and
  [MIC-VPU-01](scoreboard-control/mic-vpu-01.md): the video board controllers CTS ships.
