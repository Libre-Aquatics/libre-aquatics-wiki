This article is a stub. The All Sport 5000 series is a Daktronics keyboard console, first
released in January 2000, that runs scoreboards for many sports through interchangeable keypad
inserts, including water polo and a segment timer, and can drive displays such as the
[PC-2001](../pace-clock/pc-2001.md) pace clock by cable or by radio.

See the [scoreboard control overview](index.md) for the shared background on scoreboard
controllers.
