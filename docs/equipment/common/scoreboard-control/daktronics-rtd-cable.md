This article is a stub. The Daktronics `0A-1240-0032` is a 50 ft (15.2 m) cable with a 9-pin
male plug for the J5 RTD port of an [OmniSport 2000](../../swimming/timers/omnisport-2000.md)
and a 1/4 in plug for a [wall plate](../../swimming/deck-cabling/daktronics-wall-plate.md) or
[phone-jack junction box](daktronics-phone-jack-junction-box.md), carrying the console's
real-time data to a matrix display's controller.
