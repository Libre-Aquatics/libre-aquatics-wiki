This article is a stub. The Daktronics phone-jack junction box (`0A-1091-0227`) is a small wall
box, described in one guide as 2 × 4 in, with a 1/4 in phone jack that connects an All Sport or
OmniSport console, or a computer downloading Hy-Tek workouts, to the permanent signal wiring of
scoreboards, track displays and the [PC-2002](../pace-clock/pc-2002.md) pace clock.

See the [scoreboard control overview](index.md) for the shared background on scoreboard links.
