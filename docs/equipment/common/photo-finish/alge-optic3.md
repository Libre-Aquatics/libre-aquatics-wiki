This article is a stub. The OPTIc3 is an [ALGE-Timing](../../../vendors/alge-timing.md)
photo-finish camera with up to 30,000 lines a second in line-scan mode and a 100 frames per
second 2-D mode, sold as video backup for a [TM-SWIM](../../swimming/timers/alge-tm-swim.md)
system.

See the [photo finish overview](index.md) for the shared background on photo-finish equipment.
