This article is a stub. Scan'O'Vision is the photo-finish camera system made by
[Swiss Timing](../../../vendors/swiss-timing.md) for Omega, whose STAR model of 2008 recorded
2,000 frames per second and whose MYRIA model of 2016 records up to 10,000.

See the [photo finish overview](index.md) for the shared background that applies to every
photo-finish system.
