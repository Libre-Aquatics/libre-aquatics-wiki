A catalog of the named equipment used to run aquatic competitions, timing
consoles, lane hardware, connectors and cabling, start systems, judging hardware,
scoreboards, and the pool equipment that defines the field of play. Each entry is a
short reference: what the part is, its manufacturer part number, common names, and
a citation to its source manual.

## What lives here

Equipment is grouped by role:

- Timing hardware, consoles and their interfaces.
- Lane hardware, touchpads, pushbuttons, and deck plates.
- Connectors and cabling, the wiring that links the lanes to the console.
- Start systems, starters, lane speakers, and strobes.
- Judging hardware, diving terminals, water polo clocks.
- Displays, scoreboards and video boards.

The catalog is also grouped by discipline. Most articles so far cover swimming
hardware, listed below; the other disciplines and the shared infrastructure have
their own sections:

- [Diving equipment](diving/index.md): judges' terminals, diving scoring systems,
  and surface agitation.
- [Water polo equipment](water-polo/index.md): goals, game and shot-clock consoles,
  and deck displays.
- [Common equipment](common/index.md): scoreboards, cabling, consoles, audio, and
  data hardware used across every discipline.

## Articles

[Timers](swimming/timers/index.md), the Colorado Time Systems swim timing consoles; the
current Gen7 platform ships in two models that differ in deck cabling:

- [Gen7 Serial Timer](swimming/timers/gen7-serial.md): the connect-hub console
  (`GEN7-TMR`), for installations built around Gen7's newer cabling.
- [Gen7 Legacy Timer](swimming/timers/gen7-legacy.md): the multi-pin console
  (`GEN7-TMR-L`), a drop-in replacement that reuses existing CTS lane hardware.

[Start systems](swimming/starter/index.md), the units that begin the race,
cataloged by manufacturer in the overview; the Colorado Time Systems line comes
in two families:

- [Infinity Start System](swimming/starter/infinity-start-system.md): the
  portable entry-level starter (`INF-SSM`, discontinued 2024).
- [Infinity Pro Start System](swimming/starter/infinity-pro-start-system.md): its
  current successor (`INF-PRO`).
- [Championship Start System](swimming/starter/championship-start-system.md): the
  deck-wired full-featured starter (`SS`, discontinued), and its legacy
  predecessor the [CHAMP-SSM](swimming/starter/champ-ssm.md) generation.
- [Championship Elite Start System](swimming/starter/championship-elite-start-system.md):
  its current successor (`SSE`), with visual start signaling.

[External speakers](swimming/external-speaker/index.md), the loudspeakers a start
system drives beyond its own built-in speaker; the per-vendor articles cover the
lane speakers, and each start system's article covers its other speakers:

- [Colorado Time Systems lane speakers](swimming/external-speaker/cts.md):
  the `SP-6/45` under-block speaker.
- [Daktronics lane speakers](swimming/external-speaker/daktronics.md): the
  `306-45-54` under-block speaker.
- [International Sports Timing lane speakers](swimming/external-speaker/ist.md):
  the `SPKRBLOCK` under-block speaker system.
- [Superior Swim Timing lane speakers](swimming/external-speaker/sst.md): the
  SST lane speaker and harness.
- [Swiss Timing lane speakers](swimming/external-speaker/swiss-timing.md):
  the in-block loudspeaker and mobile loudspeaker sets.

[Touchpads](swimming/touchpad/index.md), the Colorado Time Systems touch panels that
sense each lane's finish and turn:

- [AquaGrip Touchpad](swimming/touchpad/aquagrip.md): the current air-filled panel
  (`TP-…G` / `TP-…GF`) with a patented non-slip surface, which replaced the
  discontinued Standard touchpad.

[Semi-automatic timing](swimming/semi-automatic/index.md), the timing systems that
start with the start signal and are stopped by human timers at the finish:

- [Pushbuttons](swimming/semi-automatic/pushbutton/index.md): the hand-held backup
  buttons a timer presses at the finish, including the
  [Colorado Time Systems Pushbutton](swimming/semi-automatic/pushbutton/cts.md)
  (`PB-6`, the timer's "pickle") on a 6 ft cord.
- [Dolphin Wireless Stopwatch Timing System](swimming/semi-automatic/dolphin.md): the
  Colorado Time Systems wireless stopwatch system, whose watches start together from
  the [Dolphin Starter Unit](swimming/starter/dolphin-starter-unit.md).

[Backstroke start devices](swimming/backstroke-start/index.md), the backstroke ledges
(foot wedges) that give a swimmer a non-slip foothold at the start and retract clear of
the water once the race begins:

- [Backstroke Start Device](swimming/backstroke-start/cts.md): the Colorado Time Systems
  spring-retracting foot wedge (`143081-C` / `143081-CL`).

More equipment articles are added over time.

## How entries work

Each part gets its own short article with an infobox (part number, manufacturer,
common names, source manual) beside a plain-language description. Entries are written
fresh and text-first; photographs are added only when they are the wiki's own or
used with permission, see [Sources & citations](../about/sources.md). Part of
this catalog is still to be written.
