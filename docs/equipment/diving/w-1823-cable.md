This article is a stub. The Daktronics `W-1823` is a 30 ft cable that connects the J14 or J15
judging port of an [OmniSport 2000](../swimming/timers/omnisport-2000.md) to an in-deck judging
wall plate for [JC-100](jc-100.md) consoles, or to the CAN socket of an
[RC-200 base station](rc-200-base-station.md) or RC-100 base station for wireless judging.

See the [diving equipment overview](index.md) for the shared background on judging equipment.
