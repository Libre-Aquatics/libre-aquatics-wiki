This article is a stub. The RC-200 (handheld `0A-1110-0062`) is a Daktronics 2.4 GHz wireless
hand-held controller, introduced in Daktronics' drawings in 2017 in place of the
[RC-100](rc-100.md), that runs small scoreboards and also works through a base station as a
wireless [JC-100](jc-100.md)-style judge console for the
[OmniSport 2000](../swimming/timers/omnisport-2000.md), nine to a base station.

See the [diving equipment overview](index.md) for the shared background on judging equipment.
