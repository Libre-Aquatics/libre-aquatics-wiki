This article is a stub. The JC-100 is a Daktronics hand-held console, wired over a CAN network
and powered by the device it plugs into, that judges use to enter diving and synchronized
swimming scores into an [OmniSport 2000](../swimming/timers/omnisport-2000.md) and that also
programs workouts and settings on the [PC-2001](../common/pace-clock/pc-2001.md) and
[PC-2002](../common/pace-clock/pc-2002.md) pace clocks.

See the [diving equipment overview](index.md) for the shared background on judging equipment.
