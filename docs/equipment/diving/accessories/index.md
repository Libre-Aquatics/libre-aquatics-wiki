The smaller items used with diving judging equipment: chargers, junction boxes, covers and
cables.

## What lives here

- Chargers for hand-held judge consoles.
- Wall boxes and covers that connect judge consoles to permanent wiring.
- Cables between judging equipment and a timing console.

Base stations and interface boxes are listed under [diving equipment](../index.md).

## Articles

| Item | Manufacturer or seller | Use |
|---|---|---|
| [RC-200 charging station](rc-200-charging-station.md) | Daktronics | Recharges up to six [RC-200](../rc-200.md) handhelds |
| [JC-100 junction box](jc-100-junction-box.md) | Daktronics | Connects a [JC-100](../jc-100.md) to a pace clock |
| [Judging control J-box cover](daktronics-judging-jbox-cover.md) | Daktronics | Sockets for judge consoles and a scoreboard at a wall box |
| [W-1823 cable](w-1823-cable.md) | Daktronics | Connects the judging port of an OmniSport 2000 to a wall plate or base station |

The [INT131](../../common/accessories/swiss-timing-int131.md) converter, which links Omega MTE
keypads to Swiss Timing's scoring programs in diving and artistic swimming, is with the
[common accessories](../../common/accessories/index.md).
