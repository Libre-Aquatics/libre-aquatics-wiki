This article is a stub. The Daktronics JC-100 junction box (`0A-1240-0027`) is a wall box with a
single connector, mounted beside a [PC-2002](../common/pace-clock/pc-2002.md) pace clock, that
connects a [JC-100](jc-100.md) handheld console to the clock's CAN terminals over a two-pair cable.

See the [JC-100](jc-100.md) article for the console it serves.
