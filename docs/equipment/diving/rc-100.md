This article is a stub. The RC-100 (handheld `0A-1110-0053`) is a Daktronics 900 MHz wireless
hand-held controller that reached an [OmniSport 2000](../swimming/timers/omnisport-2000.md)
through a base station and served as the wireless counterpart of the
[JC-100](jc-100.md) judge console, nine to a base station, until Daktronics' drawings moved to
the [RC-200](rc-200.md) in 2017.

See the [diving equipment overview](index.md) for the shared background on judging equipment.
