The hardware used to run a diving competition, the judging and scoring gear that
turns a panel's marks into a result, the displays that show it, and the pool-surface
equipment that supports the dive itself. Diving is a judged sport, so its equipment
centres on collecting judges' scores rather than measuring a time against a clock.

## What lives here

Diving equipment is grouped by role:

- Judges' terminals, the handheld or deck keypads each judge uses to enter a raw
  score for a dive.
- Diving scoring systems, the console and software that combine each judge's mark
  with the dive's degree of difficulty and total a round.
- Displays and scoreboards, the boards that show the current dive, the judges'
  marks, and the running standings.
- Surface agitation systems, the sparger or "bubbler" that ripples the water
  surface so divers can read its position, and the related aeration used in
  training.

Springboards, platforms, and the diving well itself are the field of play, not
equipment, and are out of scope here.

## Articles

Diving scoring hardware is made by the same timing vendors that supply swimming, listed on

their connections.

### Judging systems and terminals

- [Colorado Time Systems Gen7 wired judging](gen7-wired-judging.md): the wired judging
  system released in 2016 for Gen7 Diving and Gen7 Artistic Swimming
- [Colorado Time Systems wireless judging](wireless-judging.md): handheld judges'
  terminals and a radio interface for the System 5, System 6 and synchro software, in
  900 MHz and 2.4 GHz generations
- [Colorado Time Systems judging terminals](judging-terminals.md): the wired terminals
  for the System 5 and System 6
- [Daktronics JC-100](jc-100.md): the wired hand-held judge console for the OmniSport 2000,
  which also programs Daktronics pace clocks
- [Daktronics RC-100](rc-100.md) and [RC-200](rc-200.md): the 900 MHz and 2.4 GHz wireless
  hand-held controllers that serve as judge consoles through a base station
- [Omega MTE judge's keypad](omega-mte-keypads.md): Swiss Timing's handheld ZigBee scoring
  keypad, used with DIV Scoring Manager
