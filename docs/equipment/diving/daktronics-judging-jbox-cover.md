This article is a stub. The Daktronics judging control J-box cover (`0A-1240-0057`) finishes a
wall box at an extra judging location in an in-deck pool, giving a socket where a chain of
[JC-100](jc-100.md) judge consoles plugs in and a scoreboard socket, both wired back through the
wall to the [wall plate](../swimming/deck-cabling/daktronics-wall-plate.md) where the
[OmniSport 2000](../swimming/timers/omnisport-2000.md) connects.

See the [diving equipment overview](index.md) for the shared background on judging equipment.
