This article is a stub. Colorado Time Systems judging terminals are wired keypads, up to ten
per console, that let diving and synchronized swimming judges send their awards to a
[System 5](../swimming/timers/system-5.md) or [System 6](../swimming/timers/system-6.md)
console through a judging interface box, in place of hand-entered scores.

See the [diving equipment overview](index.md) for the shared background, and
[wireless judging](wireless-judging.md) for the radio system that followed.
