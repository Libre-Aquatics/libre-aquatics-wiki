Team management is the club/program side of competition: keeping athletes,
rosters, and best times in order, and getting entries in and out of meets cleanly.
This is where [Libre Aquatics Team](../software/libre-aquatics-team.md) is aimed.

## What lives here

- Roster and athletes, registrations, groups, and eligibility.
- Best times and standards, tracking personal bests and comparing them against
  qualifying standards.
- Entries and declarations, choosing events, submitting entry files to
  [meet management](../meet-management/index.md), and handling scratches.
- Results back, importing results to update athletes' best times.
